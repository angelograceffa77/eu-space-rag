In accordance with Article 89 of EMSA’s Financial Regulation, the relevant provisions (including Title VII on Procurement and Concessions) of the Regulation (EU, Euratom) No 2018/1046 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the financial rules applicable to the general budget of the Union shall apply to EMSA. Open procedures are advertised on the Funding and Tenders (F&T) portal ; All other types of procedures (e.g. CPNEG) are advertised in this section of the EMSA website.
