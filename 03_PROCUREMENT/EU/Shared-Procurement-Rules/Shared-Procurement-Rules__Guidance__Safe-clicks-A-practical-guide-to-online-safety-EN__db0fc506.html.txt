But they come with real risks. Online platforms may offer addictive designs: infinite scrolling, short videos with short attention span, highly personalised and targeted content. The amount of time children spend looking at screens has never been so high. And the more time they spend online, the more likely it is that they will be exposed to harmful and illegal content, and online predators. 💬 Start the conversation early The best online safety tool is real, offline communication. Talk to children regularly about what they do online, who they interact with, and what they enjoy. Show interest in what they like more, their favourite games or videos. Creating an open environment makes it more likely they'll seek help if something goes wrong. 💡 Did you know?
