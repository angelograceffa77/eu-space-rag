Democracy. Home Resources Europa Web Guide Europa Web Guide Rules and guidelines that apply to European Commission websites, covering editorial, legal, technical, visual and contractual aspects. The Europa Web Guide (EWP) is the official rulebook for the European Commission's web presence. It covers editorial, legal, technical, visual and contractual aspects of the EC online presence. All European Commission websites must observe these rules and guidelines. Everyone designing, building, creating content on the Europa domain are expected to follow these rules and to stay informed on updates. Pages comprising 'EU login required' in their title are restricted to Commission services and their internal contractors. More information about the Europa Web Guide ’s contents.
