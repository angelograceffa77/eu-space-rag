Procurement

  • General Information
  • Open/Restricted Tender Procedures
  • Low & Middle Value Negotiated Procedures

    • All open and restricted tender procedures launched by Frontex are published in the Official Journal of the European Union as well as on EU Funding & Tenders Portal where the tender documentation is available for downloading (please note that the tender dossier for restricted tender procedures is revealed to the tenderers that will pass to the second stage of the tendering process).

    If you experience a downloading problem, please send an e-mail to procurement@frontex.europa.eu indicating the reference number and title of the tender you are interested in.

    Please note that economic operators must be listed in the Participant register to be able to submit requests to participate/tenders through the F&T Portal.





    Frontex, the European Border and Coast Guard Agency, supports EU Member States and Schengen-associated countries in the management of the EU's external borders and the fight against cross-border crime.
