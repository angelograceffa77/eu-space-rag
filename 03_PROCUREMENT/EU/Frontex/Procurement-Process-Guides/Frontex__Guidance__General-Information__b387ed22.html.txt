In accordance with this Regulation the relevant provisions of the Financial Regulation applicable to the General Budget of the Union (Council Regulation (EC, Euratom) No. 2024/2509 shall apply. According to the abovementioned Regulation, Title VII - Procurement and Concessions and Annex I - Procurement, the choice of tender procedure and advertising requirements depend mainly on the threshold amount of each planned contract and are currently as follows: Value of the call for tender: < €15,000.01 Minimum procedure required Negotiated procedure with a single supplier/service provider Means of advertising No advertising Value of the call for tender: €15,000.01 - €60,000.00 Minimum procedure required Negotiated procedure with at least 3 candidates without a contract notice - Low Value Negotiated Procedure Means of advertising EU Funding & Tenders Portal Value of the call for tender: > €60,000.00 and less than €140,000 for supplies and services Minimum procedure required Negotiated procedure with at least 5 candidates without a contract notice - Middle Value Negotiated Procedure Means of advertising EU Funding & Tenders Portal Value of the call for tender: > €140,000.00 supplies and services Minimum procedure required Open tender procedure or restricted tender procedure with a contract notice published in the Official Journal Means of advertising: EU Funding & Tenders Portal Value of the call for tender: Services under Annex XIV to Directive 2014/24/EU Minimum procedure required Competitive procedure with negotiation Means of advertising E U Funding and Tenders portal Non-procurement procedures: Auction Sales Non-procurement procedures General questions related to Frontex procurement procedures shall be addressed to: procurement@frontex.europa.eu Frontex Annual Procurement Plan 2026 Frontex Annual Procurement Plan 2026 Annual Procurement Plan (269.7 KB) 2026 Amendment 1 to Annual Procurement Plan (319.9 KB) 2025 Frontex Annual Procurement Plan 2025 Amendment 2 to Frontex Annual Procurement Plan (337.1 KB) 2025 Amendment 1 to Frontex Annual Procurement Plan (1.9 MB) 2025 Frontex Annual Procurement Plan (2.4 MB) Frequently Asked Questions Frequently Asked Questions document Frequently Asked Questions (147.9 KB) Frontex, the European Border and Coast Guard Agency, supports EU Member States and Schengen-associated countries in the management of the EU's external borders and the fight against cross-border crime.
