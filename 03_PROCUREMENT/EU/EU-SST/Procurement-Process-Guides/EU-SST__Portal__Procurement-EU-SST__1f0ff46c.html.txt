Open procedures Title Description Submission deadline More information Prior Information Notice – Space Traffic Coordination Platform (STCP) Through the creation of an EU Platform for Space Traffic Coordination, the initiative should seek to structure, secure and expand coordination between satellite operators, public entities and commercial SSA providers, building on existing services developed under the EU Space Surveillance and Tracking (EU SST). EUSPA notice Past procedures Title Description More information Augmentation of re-entry events prediction through the analysis of optical sensor data gathered from a worldwide network of telescopes The subject of the contract concerns the provision of expert services aimed at increasing the use of astrometric observations to improve the performance and accuracy of the Re-entry Analysis (RE) service implemented as part of the EU’s Space Surveillance and Tracking (SST) system. TED contract notice Consultation on the need and definition of a marketplace for space surveillance and tracking services (SST) This consultation concerns the potential need to establish a marketplace in Europe to connect companies selling services with potential customers that has been identified. This platform could serve to boost and energise the commercial sector around space surveillance and facilitate communications between European companies, positioning them in a competitive environment. Contract notice Coordinated planning of the EU Space Surveillance and Tracking (EU SST) sensors network This procedure concerns operation and maintenance activities for the coordinated planning service of the EU Space Surveillance and Tracking (EU SST) sensors network. TED contract notice Development and maintenance of the GSSAC Mission System Framework The overarching objective of this project is to ensure the continuous operation of the German Space Situational Awareness Centre (GSSAC) Mission System, which enables the Centre to fulfil its mandate. TED contract notice Establishment of a database and accompanying scripts for SENTIPLA This procedure concerns the service of creating a database and accompanying scripts. The main tasks of the system will include downloading, sharing and storing information about the characteristics of a group of devices (sensors), as well as managing system users, their grouping and permissions to view and modify the data contained in the system. TED contract notice EU SST - Cataloguing The objective of the project is to foster the operational capabilities of the EU SST Catalogue. This includes operational support and further development of the Catalogue, as well as R&D activities dealing with cislunar cataloguing, synthetic covariance generation and advanced uncertainty modelling. TED contract notice EU SST - Debris population The aim of this activity is to provide expertise to EU SST in the field of space environment evolution and to support mainly the activities of the Expert Team System Evolution. TED contract notice EU SST - Optimising radar observations The objective of this project is to investigate two possibilities for the use of radar sensors that could potentially improve the provision of EU SST services: the added value of using radars in beam park or spotlight mode for assessing fragmentation events, and the added value that radars can contribute to the EU SST catalogue. TED contract notice EU SST - R&D on architecture studies - Improvement of the CNES' simulation bench to build up an optimised network of sensors contributing to SST activities This competitive bidding procedure concerns the evolution of the French SST simulation bench (BAS3E), used to assess the theoretical performances of a sensors network contributing to SST activities. TED contract notice EU SST - SBSS In this activity the contractor shall research options to enhance the performance of a Space Based Surveillance System (SBSS) with regard to providing SST data (i.e. data quality, quantity, timeliness) by improving its on-board processing capabilities. TED contract notice EU SST - Secure data sharing with Quantum Keys over long distances, using space-based facilities The contract includes the utilisation of space infrastructures to demonstrate, for the first time, a quantum-secured interconnection between QKD-enabled Optical Ground Stations (OGSs) / EU SST observation nodes separated by distances exceeding 1000 kilometres. TED contract notice EU SST – Architecture studies This consultation concerns services related to architecture studies within the EU SST Partnership, as defined in the technical specifications. TED contract notice EU SST – Collision Avoidance service operations, maintenance and evolution support This consultation concerns services related to the CNES operational centre for space surveillance COSE (Centre Opérationnel de Surveillance de l’Espace). TED contract notice EU SST – Commercial data provision This consultation aims at the acquisition of commercial data within the framework of the EU SST programme of the European Commission. This involves the integration of already operational commercial sensors, based on known technologies (radars, optical sensors and passive telemetry sensors, laser telemetry and other suitable sensors) installed either on the ground or in space. TED contract notice EU SST – Commercial data provision 2 This consultation concerns services related to commercial data provision, as defined in the technical specifications. TED contract notice EU SST – Development of innovative optical commercial sensors This is a call for proposals for projects in order to finance the development of sensors or upgrades of sensors owned by EU companies, through financial support to third parties (cascade funding) under the EUSST2023-26 grant of the EU SST Partnership. ASI contract notice EU SST – Horizon Scanning – Core study The aim of this assignment is to develop future scenarios from which possible medium and long-term challenges for EU SST can be crystallised. The activities of Horizon Scanning thus serve strategic foresight. The areas of the study concern current issues at the interface of global space policy, infrastructure and the operation of SST, both from a forward-looking (future-oriented) and a horizontal (across comparable areas) perspective. TED contract notice EU SST – Horizon Scanning study – Counterspace capabilities Horizon Scanning refers to studies carried out as a forward-looking effort to identify, observe and understand emerging trends, potential issues and long-term developments. The activity aims at systematically processing and making sense of them in an explorative setting. This study will deal with counterspace capabilities and their impact on the orbital environment and SST. TED contract notice EU SST – Horizon Scanning study – Implementation of political space ambition Horizon Scanning refers to studies carried out as a forward-looking effort to identify, observe and understand emerging trends, potential issues and long-term developments. The activity aims at systematically processing and making sense of them in an explorative setting. This study will deal with the implementation of political space ambition, particularly in the SST domain. TED contract notice EU SST – Horizon Scanning study – New orbits Space beyond the Earth's orbits is becoming increasingly relevant, for instance for planetary research, solar observation or commercial purposes. There are also new opportunities to utilise locations and orbits for observing space objects orbiting the Earth; more specifically, this concerns locations on other celestial bodies and the corresponding orbits (e.g. lunar and cis-lunar), as well as the Lagrange points and perhaps other positions in space. The benefits, challenges and opportunities associated with the various aspects of this development will be identified and discussed within this study, which will also look at these new locations and orbits with a view to identify possible scenarios for long-term development and exploitation; thus, the potential impact on the EU SST system and mandate shall be examined. TED contract notice EU SST – Horizon Scanning study – SST market scanning and influence of EU SST data on commercialisation The evolution of EU SST benefits from improved capabilities and leverages solutions offered on the commercial market. This study will examine the potential impact of EU SST data and services on the commercial sector. It will also look at the services that are essential for the safety of the space environment and could potentially be jeopardised by commercialisation. TED contract notice EU SST – Identification of objects potentially hazardous to space activities and determination of space capability The purpose of this contract is to analyse, develop and apply to the field of space surveillance a series of indices applied to the space environment that represent an effective and efficient way of evaluating the impact of a given mission in space. TED contract notice EU SST – Innovative commercial sensors This is a call for proposals for projects in order to finance the development of sensors or upgrades of sensors owned by EU companies, through financial support to third parties (cascade funding) under the EUSST2023-26 grant of the EU SST Partnership. EU SST – Performance monitoring tool The aim of this activity is to develop a software tool that will support operational performance monitoring in EU SST. TED contract notice EU SST – Preparing the monitoring compliance with mitigation standards The aim of this project is to develop a software environment that can be used to carry out statistical data analyses on compliance with space debris avoidance measures and other parameters related to the condition of man-made objects in Earth’s orbit (satellites, rocket upper stages, space debris) on the basis of various data sources and additional research. TED contract notice EU SST – R&D on Attitude Estimation This procedure concerns services related to activities on Attitude Estimation in order to find innovative ways to better describe the attitude of uncooperative space objects, by harnessing all the information available on the current and projected EU SST sensor capacity. TED contract notice EU SST – R&D on catalogue impact of space weather This R&D activity shall investigate the effect of space weather events on the cataloguing capabilities of the EU SST Catalogue. More specifically, their impact and potential ways to reduce these impacts with regard to correlation, orbit propagation and therefore catalogue build-up and maintenance shall be investigated. TED contract notice EU SST – R&D on cataloguing algorithms The aim of this activity is to gain insights into how the quality and quantity of the EU SST Catalogue, as well as the computational performance of the system, can be increased using different algorithms and models and to configure the processing chain accordingly. TED contract notice EU SST – R&D on Collision Avoidance methods This consultation concerns services related to research and development to improve Collision Avoidance methods, as defined in the technical specifications. TED contract notice EU SST – R&D on covariance estimation and improvement for risk analysis In the collision avoidance field, the uncertainties are supposed Gaussian and their mathematical representation is called “Covariance”. Good estimations of the covariance are crucial to avoid misclassification of the risk. This procedure concerns services related to the study of a better estimation of the covariance in various contexts. TED contract notice EU SST – R&D on DLT added-value analysis This competitive bidding procedure concerns services related to activities on assessing the added value of Distributed Ledger Technologies (DLTs) within the EU SST context. TED contract notice EU SST – R&D on laser contribution This activity shall demonstrate the capabilities of laser ranging in the context of SST to achieve the best operational added value in the future. TED contract notice EU SST – R&D on object size estimation This R&D activity shall investigate whether object size estimation based on observation and catalogue data is possible and operationally usable with regard to correlation, orbit propagation, and catalogue maintenance and build-up. TED contract notice EU SST – R&D on quantitative assessment of Conjunction Data Messages for robust collision risk estimate This competitive bidding procedure concerns services related to R&D aiming to enhance the viability of the products produced by the EU SST Collision Avoidance service. TED contract notice EU SST – R&D on RFI This procedure concerns services related to R&D activities on Radio-Frequency Interferences (RFI) in the context of EU SST. TED contract notice EU SST – R&D on Space Based Surveillance System This procedure concerns services related to tasks to be performed for the development of disruptive SBSS solutions (through primary, secondary payloads or the use of non-dedicated equipment) contributing to SST activities and leading to the production of an engineering model, as defined in the technical specifications. TED contract notice EU SST – R&D on the preparation of a responsive architecture for STM activities The objective of this R&D activity is to find innovative ways to tackle challenges related to Space Traffic Management (STM), within the EU SST framework. TED contract notice EU SST – Secure network study The study is intended to prepare the future introduction of a secure network at EU SST level. TED contract notice EU SST – Services for activities related to the prevention of in-orbit collisions The purpose of this contract is to conduct studies, analyses, and proof-of-concept tests on the various steps involved in providing the Collision Avoidance service. TED contract notice Expertise on Space Environment Evolution for EU SST The aim of this activity is to provide expertise to EU SST in the field of Space Environment Evolution and to support mainly the activities of the Expert Team System Evolution. TED contract notice Operation and maintenance services for optical sensors used in surveillance and tracking of artificial space objects orbiting the Earth in LEO/MEO/GEO orbits This procedure concerns operation and maintenance services for Romanian optical sensors used for data acquisition and processing. TED contract notice Operation and maintenance services for optical sensors used in surveillance and tracking of artificial space objects orbiting the Earth in LEO/MEO/GEO orbits – v2 This procedure concerns operation and maintenance services for Romanian optical sensors used for data acquisition and processing. TED contract notice Prior Information Notice – Enhanced SST services through data sharing and networking This market information notice concerns services related to the development of algorithms for the characterisation of targets observed with EU SST sensors, and the development of procedures for tasking sensors within the EU SST network. INAF notice Provision of service for performance monitoring and continuous calibration activities for the EU SST sensors network This procedure concerns the provision of a service for carrying out tasks related to the monitoring of the EU SST sensors network, including Spanish sensors, as well as the continuous calibration of the sensors. TED contract notice Provision of services for the operation and maintenance of S3TOC This procedure concerns the provision of services for the operation and maintenance of the Spanish SST Operations Centre (S3TOC). The Spanish Space Agency (AEE) has committed to these tasks as a beneficiary of the EUSST2023-26 grant funded by the EU Space Regulation. TED contract notice R&D on secure data exchange via the EU SST Database The aim of this procedure is to study which modern technologies can be applied to ensure a higher degree of traceability in the EU SST data flow. To confirm a successful exchange of data a trusted, practical and secure data sharing system or procedure is needed. It shall be evaluated if and how the security of the data exchange can be improved by certain technologies; encryption and secure communication frameworks shall be assessed for this purpose. TED contract notice Refactoring and maintenance of the EU SST Database and related processes The aim of this activity is to support the team of the German Space Situational Awareness Centre (GSSAC/WRLageZ) in the further development, maintenance and operation of the EU SST Database. TED contract notice Secured data sharing using a Quantum Key Distribution network The contract includes the study, design, deployment and demonstration of a quantum secured interconnection between the GR_KRYONERI and GR_NOC nodes, both of which are part of EU SST and belong to the National Observatory of Athens. Statement of Work Service to conduct a study on requirements for secure communications between sensors and operations centres This study will aim at preparing for the upcoming deployment of a secure network between sensors and operations centres at national level, taking into account the requirements of EU SST. TED contract notice Services of observation, provision and processing of astronomical data from the CHEIA radar This procurement procedure mainly concerns data acquisition as well as the operation and maintenance of the CHEIA radar in the context of EU SST. TED contract notice Space debris laser ranging operations in support of the EU Space Surveillance and Tracking (EU SST) The aim of this activity is to commission a Laser Ranging Station to carry out measurements on an operational basis, whereby the data obtained from the measurements should make a complementary contribution to the services defined within the framework of EU SST and in particular to the EU SST Catalogue. Contract notice Space debris laser ranging operations in support of the EU Space Surveillance and Tracking (EU SST) 2 The aim of this invitation to tender is to commission a Laser Ranging Station to carry out operational measurements that should complement the services defined for EU SST, and especially the EU SST catalogue of orbital debris.
