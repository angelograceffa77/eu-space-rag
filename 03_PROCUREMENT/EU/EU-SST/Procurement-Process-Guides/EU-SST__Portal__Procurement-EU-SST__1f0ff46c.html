EU SST – Horizon Scanning – Core study

The aim of this assignment is to develop future scenarios from which possible medium and long-term challenges for EU SST can be crystallised.  TED contract notice
EU SST – Horizon Scanning study – Counterspace capabilities

Horizon Scanning refers to studies carried out as a forward-looking effort to identify, observe and understand emerging trends, potential issues and long-term developments. The activity aims at systematically processing and making sense of them in an explorative setting. This study will deal with counterspace capabilities and their impact on the orbital environment and SST.

 TED contract notice
EU SST – Horizon Scanning study – Implementation of political space ambition

Horizon Scanning refers to studies carried out as a forward-looking effort to identify, observe and understand emerging trends, potential issues and long-term developments. The activity aims at systematically processing and making sense of them in an explorative setting. This study will deal with the implementation of political space ambition, particularly in the SST domain.

 TED contract notice
EU SST – Horizon Scanning study – New orbits

Space beyond the Earth's orbits is becoming increasingly relevant, for instance for planetary research, solar observation or commercial purposes. There are also new opportunities to utilise locations and orbits for observing space objects orbiting the Earth; more specifically, this concerns locations on other celestial bodies and the corresponding orbits (e.g. lunar and cis-lunar), as well as the Lagrange points and perhaps other positions in space. The benefits, challenges and opportunities associated with the various aspects of this development will be identified and discussed within this study, which will also look at these new locations and orbits with a view to identify possible scenarios for long-term development and exploitation; thus, the potential impact on the EU SST system and mandate shall be examined.

 TED contract notice
EU SST – Horizon Scanning study – SST market scanning and influence of EU SST data on commercialisation

The evolution of EU SST benefits from improved capabilities and leverages solutions offered on the commercial market. This study will examine the potential impact of EU SST data and services on the commercial sector. It will also look at the services that are essential for the safety of the space environment and could potentially be jeopardised by commercialisation.
