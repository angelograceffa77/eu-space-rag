Welcome to the Georgian electronic Government Procurement (Ge-GP) system – the official portal of State Procurement in Georgia. It ensures open, transparent and competitive environment for any participant of state procurement procedures. Ge-GP system is operated by the State Procurement Agency.

The registration at the Ge-GP system is obligatory only for the procuring entities and the suppliers.
