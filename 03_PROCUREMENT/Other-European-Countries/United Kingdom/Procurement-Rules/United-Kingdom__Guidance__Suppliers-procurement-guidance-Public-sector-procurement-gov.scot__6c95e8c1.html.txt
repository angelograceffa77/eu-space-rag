Supplier Journey The Supplier Journey contains guidance for suppliers on how to bid for public sector goods, services and health and social care contracts. Supplier Development Programme The Supplier Development Programme provides a range of free tender training workshops, aimed at helping small and medium sized enterprises (SMEs) and third sector organisations who are interested in bidding for public sector contracts. Training is delivered through workshops and webinars. Courses include finding and responding to opportunities, Single Procurement Document (SPD) , improving your bid score, and understanding community benefits and sustainability. Single Point of Enquiry (SpoE) service The SPoE offers an independent, impartial and confidential service for businesses which have concerns about a procurement exercise carried out by a Scottish public body. It can work with buying organisations to try to resolve concerns in an informal manner. Find out more about the SpoE service Public Procurement Strategy for Scotland The Public Procurement Strategy for Scotland identifies supplier development as a key enabler, which underpins the successful delivery of strategic objectives and broadening good practice. Supplier and supply chain development is considered fundamental to the successful delivery of procurement that delivers positive outcomes for communities, society and organisations (public bodies, business and the third sector) Suppliers are advised to refer to the Public Procurement Strategy for Scotland in order to better understand public bodies’ priorities for their procurement activity.
