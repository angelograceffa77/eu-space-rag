MANUAL ON PUBLIC PROCUREMENT

REVIEW PROCEDURE FOR ECONOMIC OPERATORS

The manual should be useful primarily to employees and officials from the contracting authorities, but also to anybody who is not involved on a daily basis in public procurement, but still needs to receive professional information in this area. Thus, this guide may be seen also as an opportunity for judges, students, and journalists to be acquainted with public procurement procedures.

 MANUAL ON PUBLIC PROCUREMENT

REVIEW PROCEDURE FOR CONTRACTING AUTHORITIES

The manual is written from the perspective of the contracting authorities helping them to understand what are consequences of using by economic operators their right to submit a complaint and challenge the relevant decisions of the contracting authorities and what is the role of contracting authorities in the process of review of those complaints.
