Lýsing The project examines how Nordic countries address the EU’s targets for increasing net greenhouse gas removals in the land use sector under the LULUCF Regulation, while also meeting biodiversity goals in the Nature Restoration Regulation. Many ecosystems in the EU are in poor condition, but protecting and restoring them can boost carbon sinks.
