Heiti Nordic overview of instruments, measures and barriers to achieving the objectives of the LULUCF Regulation
Nr. 10540
Útboðsformur Útboð
Stutt lýsing The project will assess how Nordic countries balance climate and biodiversity goals under the EU’s LULUCF and Nature Restoration Regulations. It will examine synergies and conflicts in policy design and implementation to increase carbon removals while protecting ecosystems.
Lýsing

The project examines how Nordic countries address the EU’s targets for increasing net greenhouse gas removals in the land use sector under the LULUCF Regulation, while also meeting biodiversity goals in the Nature Restoration Regulation. Many ecosystems in the EU are in poor condition, but protecting and restoring them can boost carbon sinks.
