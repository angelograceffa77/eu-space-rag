ESO was present at the BSBF2024, the Big Science Business Forum in Trieste, Italy, from 1 to 4 October 2024: BSBF2024 ESO will be present at the BSBF2026, the Big Science Business Forum in Maastricht, Netherlands, from 28 to 30 October 2026: BSBF2026 ESO Procurement Portal Since 7 November 2022, ESO is running its new procurements through an electronic tendering tool hosted by In-Tend. Any company that wants to participate in ESO procurements will have to register on this tool. If you have previously been registered in the ESO suppliers data base, your supplier account should have been transferred to the tool automatically. To check this, please navigate to the below link and use the reset password feature to gain access: https://in-tendhost.co.uk/eso/aspx/Home You will find a guide “ Suppliers. How to Register and modify your data and contact details ” at the bottom of the page: https://in-tendhost.co.uk/eso/aspx/BuyerProfiles ANNUAL PROCUREMENT PLANNING (APP) The ESO 2026 APP listing the Procurement processes planned, as of December 2025, to be published by ESO in 2026 is available on the ESO Procurement Portal: https://in-tendhost.co.uk/eso/aspx/BuyerProfiles More details will become available when the respective Projects are published on the Tenders’ page of our Procurement Portal: https://in-tendhost.co.uk/eso/aspx/Tenders/Forthcoming . Until then, ESO regrets to have to decline requests for information, due to lack of resources. Thank you for your understanding and please stay tuned.
