HOW TO READ IT
1. Entity references: direct agency rules, conditions or supplier guidance; verify the edition and applicability.
2. National/shared references: relevant starting points, not a determination that every buyer or contract uses that regime.
3. Discovery pages and linked candidates: help find information, but have not been verified as procedural guidance.
4. Missing/download failures: explicit gaps, not evidence that no rules exist.

Use the original PDF/HTML/DOCX for citation. UTF-8 .txt companions support searching; extraction may lose tables or formatting. The catalogue records source URLs, retrieval time and SHA-256 checksums. Existing files are indexed in place. Their original URLs and currency are not assumed.

LEGAL AND RETRIEVAL DISTINCTIONS
Keep enacted law, agency regulations, non-binding guidance, template conditions and tender-specific documents separate. National law is not automatically the governing regime for international organisations. EU directives require national implementation and do not replace every agency's financial/procurement rules. Check the tender's own registration, eligibility, exclusion, security, submission, award and review requirements. A company match does not establish eligibility.

Check amendments, effective dates, transitional arrangements and the relevant contract date. Older translations, thresholds, historical notices and draft instruments must not be presented as current law. Some official guidance itself is old. For example, the Swedish competition authority warns its English law translation is outdated; the Czech English law page labels the original text as unamended. UK devolved procedures need separate checks. National guidance does not resolve restricted defence procurement or unpublished internal manuals.

SCOPE AND LIMITS
The inventory covers the 125 configured national source routes, standalone authority groups, extended jurisdictions, commercial Airbus routes and 57 buyer labels in the current master. Source routes are not necessarily legal entities; joint buyer labels are preserved. This is not an enumeration of every incidental organisation mentioned in all historical notices, logs, tender attachments or archived files. No claim is made that every entity now has a complete or legally current manual. Coverage status and failures identify what remains.
