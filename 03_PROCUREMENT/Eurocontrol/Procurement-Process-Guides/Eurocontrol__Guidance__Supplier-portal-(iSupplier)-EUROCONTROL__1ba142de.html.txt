Log in Register This is part of Procurement The supplier portal is a secure online tool which allows registered suppliers to: search for active negotiations by setting the NEGOTIATION search criteria to ECTL and the INVITATION RECEIVED search criteria to ‘blanc’ (empty) in the supplier portal in order to view all active negotiations; download documents about an on-going competition published on the portal; submit an offer to a competition, whether it is a call for tender, price enquiry or call for interest; access information related to EUROCONTROL’s procurement cycle. For all public competitions, an e-mail notification will be sent to all registered suppliers to the email address your supplier’s organisation had provided. For all restricted competitions (i.e. for call for tenders under a Framework Contract for which the supplier’s organisation has been selected), an invitation will be sent to suppliers. Suppliers are kindly invited to also verify any emails received in their email ‘spam’ folders to avoid the risk of emails having been re-routed there due to the supplier’s organisation’s email policies.
