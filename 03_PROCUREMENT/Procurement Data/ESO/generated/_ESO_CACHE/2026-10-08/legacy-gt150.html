ADVANCE INFORMATION ON FORTHCOMING CALLS FOR TENDERS/PRELIMINARY INQUIRIES

Since 7 November 2022, ESO is running its new procurements through an electronic tendering tool hosted by In-Tend. Any company that wants to participate in ESO procurements will have to register on this tool.

If you have previously been registered in the ESO suppliers data base, your supplier account should have been transferred to the tool automatically. To check this, please navigate to the below link and use the reset password feature to gain access:

https://in-tendhost.co.uk/eso/aspx/Home

You will find a guide “Suppliers. How to Register and modify your data and contact details” at the bottom of the page: https://in-tendhost.co.uk/eso/aspx/BuyerProfiles

Below is the list of procurements carried out by ESO in the past:  Calls for Tenders and Preliminary Inquiries expected to exceed 150,000 EUR.

For new procurements, please visit the new ESO Procurement Portal, using the link mentioned above.

For information or questions, please contact cp-garching@eso.org for Europe or cp-chile@eso.org for Chile.
