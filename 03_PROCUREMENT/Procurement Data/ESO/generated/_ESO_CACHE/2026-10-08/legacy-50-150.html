FORTHCOMING PRICE INQUIRIES / CALLS FOR TENDERS / REQUEST FOR INFORMATION from 50,000 EUR to under 150,000 EUR

Since 7 November 2022, ESO is running its new procurements through an electronic tendering tool hosted by In-Tend. Any company that wants to participate in ESO procurements will have to register on this tool.

If you have previously been registered in the ESO suppliers database, your supplier account should have been transferred to the tool automatically. To check this, please navigate to the following link and use the Forgotten Details feature to gain access: 

https://in-tendhost.co.uk/eso/aspx/Home

Any company that wants to participate to an ESO procurement has to register on that tool. You will find a guide “Suppliers. How to Register and modify your data and contact details” at the bottom of the page: https://in-tendhost.co.uk/eso/aspx/BuyerProfiles

Below is the list of procurements carried out by ESO in the past:
Price Inquiries, Calls for Tenders and Request for Information from 50,000 EUR to under 150,000 EUR.

For new procurements, please visit the new ESO Procurement Portal, using the link mentioned above.

For information or questions, please contact cp-garching@eso.org for Europe or cp-chile@eso.org for Chile.

PR 111547 03.05.2022

Price inquiry (PQ) for the production of six pyramid prisms. The scope of work includes the manufacturing, polishing, coating, and testing of 6 special prisms made out of the low dispersion glass S-FPL55. The prisms shall have a dimension of approximately 30 mm diameter and a height of 10 mm. The prims shall have a pyramid shape on one side, i. e. four facets converging in a tip under a shallow angle of approx. 4 degrees. The pyramid edge and tip shall be sharp with a thickness of 10 micron or less within the central few millimetres around the tip of the prism. The prism backside shall be flat. The final prisms shall receive an AR coating (wavelength range 1.4 – 2.3 micron).

In order to facilitate and verify the challenging edge sharpness, the contract has two steps. Phase 1: The production of two un-coated prototype prisms, which demonstrate the edge sharpness and full compliance with the specifications. Phase 2: In case of compliant proto-types, the remaining four prisms shall be manufactured and all six prisms shall receive the AR coating.

Contract is expected to be awarded in Q2/2022. The expected lead time of the delivery is 12 months for the whole project after contract kick-off.
