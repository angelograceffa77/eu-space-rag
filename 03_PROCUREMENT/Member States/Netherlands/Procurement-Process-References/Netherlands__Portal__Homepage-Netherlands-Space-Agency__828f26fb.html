Netherlands Space Agency

The Netherlands Space Agency is the space agency of the national government. We connect societal challenges with the opportunities space provides and guide the development of knowledge, capabilities, and collaboration in the Netherlands. In doing so, we contribute to a resilient and prosperous society.
