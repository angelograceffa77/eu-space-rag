  1. Act CXLIII of 2015 on Public Procurement

Act CXLIII of 2015 on Public Procurement

(This Act was adopted by the National Assembly at its meeting of 22 September 2015.)

The English translation of the legal provisions is provided by the Public Procurement Authority of Hungary solely for informational purposes. The Authority shall not take any responsibility for the accuracy and completeness of the translations. The Hungarian version published in the Hungarian Official Gazette (Magyar Közlöny) shall be considered the authentic text.

For the sake of the transparency and public control of the effective use of public funds, in order to establish conditions of fair competition in public procurement, furthermore, to enhance access of local small and medium-sized enterprises to procurement procedures, to promote environmental protection and social considerations of the State - in accordance with the international agreements concluded by Hungary in the field of public procurement and the directives of the European Union - the National Assembly of Hungary has adopted the following Act:

  • PART ONE - GENERAL PART
    • CHAPTER I - PRINCIPLES AND SCOPE OF THE ACT (1-2. §)
    • CHAPTER II - DEFINITIONS (3. §)
    • CHAPTER III - SCOPE OF THE ACT (4-24. §)
    • CHAPTER IV - RRULES PERTAINING TO THE PARTICIPANTS OF PROCUREMENT PROCEDURES (25-36. §)
    • CHAPTER V - PUBLICATION, COMMUNICATION, THE PUBLIC NATURE OF PROCUREMENT PROCEDURES (37-48. §)
  • PART TWO - PROVISIONS RELATED TO PUBLIC PROCUREMENTS REACHING EU THRESHOLDS
    • CHAPTER VI - STARTING OF PROCUREMENT PROCEDURES (49-52. §)
    • CHAPTER VII - THE BINDING NATURE OF THE PARTIIES’ DECLARATIONS IN PROCUREMENT PROCEDURES (53-55. §)
    • CHAPTER VIII - ADDTITIONAL INFORMATION AND ON-THE-SITE INSPECTION (56. §)
    • CHAPTER IX - PROCUREMENT DOCUMENTS (57. §)
    • CHAPTER X - REQUIREMENTS REGARDING THE SUBJECT-MATTER OF THE PUBLIC PROCUREMENT (58-61. §)
    • CHAPTER XI - REQUIREMENTS RELATED TO TENDERERS AND CANDIDATES (62-65. §)
    • CHAPTER XII - TENDERS AND REQUESTS TO PARTICIPATE (66-68. §)
    • CHAPTER XIII - THE EVALUATION OF TENDERS AND REQUESTS TO PARTICIPATE (69-74. §)
    • CHAPTER XIV - THE RESULTS OF THE PROCUREMENT PROCEDURE (75-80. §)
    • CHAPTER XIV/A - SPECIAL RULES SPECIFIED BY EUROPEAN UNION LEGAL INSTRUMENTS
    • CHAPTER XV - SPECIFIC RULES PERTAINING TO CERTAIN TYPES OF PROCEDURE (81-103. §)
    • CHAPTER XVI - SPECIFIC PROCUREMENT METHODS (104-109. §)
  • PART THREE - PROCEDURES BELOW EU THRESHOLDS
    • CHAPTER XVII - RULES PERTAINING TO THE PROCEDURES BELOW EU THRESHOLDS (110-117. §)
  • PART FOUR - CONCESSION AWARD PROCEDURES
    • CHAPTER XVIII - TTHE RULES PERTAINING TO CONCESSION AWARD PROCEDURES (118-128. §)
    • CHAPTER XIX - SPECIAL RULES PERTAINING TO CONCESSIONS HAVING A PARTICULAR SUBJECT-MATTER (129. §)
  • PART FIVE - RULES PERTAINING TO CONTRACTS
    • CHAPTER XX - RULES PERTAINING TO CONTRACTS (130-143. §)
  • PART SIX - JUDICIAL REMEDIES AVAILABLE FOR PUBLIC PROCUREMENT
    • CHAPTER XXI - RULES PERTAINING TO REVIEW PROCEDURES (144-177. §)
    • CHAPTER XXII - PROCEDURE OF THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION (178. §)
  • PART SEVEN - THE PUBLIC PROCUREMENT AUTHORITY AND THE PUBLIC PROCUREMENT ARBITRATION BOARD
    • CHAPTER XXIII - THE PUBLIC PROCUREMENT AUTHORITY (179-191. §)
    • CHAPTER XXIV - THE PUBLIC PROCUREMENT ARBITRATION BOARD (192-193. §)
  • PART EIGHT - FINAL PROVISIONS (194-201. §)
    • FINAL PROVISIONS (194-201. §)
