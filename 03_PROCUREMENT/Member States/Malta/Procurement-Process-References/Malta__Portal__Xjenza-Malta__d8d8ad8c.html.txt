Xjenza Malta Get in Touch Discover more Contact Us Discover more Welcome to Xjenza Malta Xjenza Malta is the government agency responsible for promoting and coordinating scientific research, technological innovation, and science communication in Malta. Explore Our Main Services Policy & Strategy The Policy & Strategy Unit (PSU) leads the development of the National R&I Strategy, oversees its implementation and follows up on its monitoring. The PSU supports the government in the development of Malta’s Smart Specialisation Strategy and provides technical support to the governance structure set up for its implementation. Read More R&I Programmes The Research & Innovation Unit (R&I) manages three national funding streams by way of direct implementation support to the National R&I Strategic Plan 2023 – 2027: Fusion, Thematic programmes and the Research Networking Scheme. Read More Internationalisation The Internationalisation Unit supports scientific-research expert stakeholders based in Malta to actively engage in transnational Research and Innovation (R&I) projects by developing, managing and/or facilitating their participation in various bilateral, regional and international funding programmes. Read More Framework Programme – Horizon Europe The Framework Programme Unit serves as the National Contact Point and provides guidance to all Maltese expert stakeholders interested in applying for Horizon Europe funding. Horizon Europe is the current EU Framework Programme (2021-2027), which has a budget of €95.5 billion. Horizon Europe is the EU’s key funding programme for research and innovation for Maltese researchers and organisations. Read More Space The Space Unit, through the Plan for European Cooperating States (PECS), makes Malta part of a larger pan-European network that fosters growth, development and broader collaboration opportunities to develop a thriving local Space sector. Read More Plumtri provides an opportunity for researchers to connect and network with one another, while gaining access to relevant information on a multitude of R&I developments, events and funding opportunities Esplora, Malta’s Interactive Science Centre, is an informal educational and recreational facility suited to visitors of all age groups.
