Electronic Tendering - Welcome to e-PPS Advanced search Log in Welcome to e-PPS × For enjoying the full functionality of this application, please ensure that Javascript is enabled in your browser. e-PPS is a web-based, collaborative system to facilitate the full lifecycle of a tendering process, for both buyers and suppliers. It offers a secure, interactive, dynamic environment for procurements of any nature, complexity or value (above or below EU thresholds), enforcing (where appropriate) and encouraging recognised best practices. e-PPS supports the process of procuring works, services and supplies electronically. All public procurement procedures foreseen by the law are supported for both one-off or repetitive purchases through several dedicated sub-modules providing facilities for user registration, competition notification, tender preparation and submission, online tender evaluation, upholding of auctions, contract awarding, contract management, creation and management of catalogues, placement of electronic orders, and much more. e-PPS can support the effort for achieving efficiency gains, while promotes core principles of the laws and regulations on public procurement such as transparency, security, availability, non-discrimination and equality of treatment.
