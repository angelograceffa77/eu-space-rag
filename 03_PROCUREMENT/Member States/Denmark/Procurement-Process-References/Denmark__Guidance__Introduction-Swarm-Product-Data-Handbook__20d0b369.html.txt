Swarm Product Data Handbook powered by earth online Handbook Introduction Product Definition and format Documentation Catalogue Swarm mission page Swarm mission page European Space Agency Terms and conditions Cookie Notice Introduction - Swarm Product Data Handbook Introduction The Swarm Product Data Handbook provides detailed descriptions of the Level 1b and Level 2 Swarm products. These products are derived from measurements from each of the Swarm spacecraft, as well as those from other spacecraft (so-called Multimission data) which are processed and delivered through the Swarm framework. Level 1b products are lower-level and more directly related to the original measurements, after significant correction and calibration. Level 2 products are typically higher-level and present derived quantities and models. Some products draw on data from multiple spacecraft, and some from multiple missions as well as ground-based observations.
