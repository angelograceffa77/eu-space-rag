If an economic operator considers that a contracting authority has violated regulations that significantly affect or might affect the awarding of the public contract, a request for legal protection in public procurement procedures may be filed in respect of any action on the part of the contracting authority in a public procurement procedure at all stages of the public procurement procedure. Content Legislation Legal protection shall be provided in: the pre-review procedure , which takes place before the contracting authority, the review procedure , which takes place before the National Review Commission, and first-instance court proceedings , which take place before the district court.
