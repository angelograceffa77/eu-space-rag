Environment and spatial planning

Space

The space in our country is the foundation for the development of our nation and the entire population of the country, the strengthening of our national identity, the prudent exploitation of spatial conditions, and the preservation of landscape diversity and natural quality. By promoting and directing spatial development, we aim to generate growth and enhance social well-being and the freedom of individuals while preserving the environment, landscape diversity and natural resources.
