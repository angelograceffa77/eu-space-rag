Whereas: 
(1) Iceland is participating in the European Geostationary Navigation Overlay Service 
(EGNOS) and Galileo components, and the space weather events (SWE) and near-Earth 
objects (NEO) subcomponents of the Space Situational Awareness (SSA) component, of 
the Union Space Programme, established by Regulation (EU) 2021/696 of the European 
Parliament and of the Council1. 
(2) Article 7(2), first subparagraph, point (b), of Regulation (EU) 2021/696 provides for the 
participation in the GOVSATCOM component of the Union Space Programme of 
members of the European Free Trade Association (EFTA) which are members of the 
European Economic Area (EEA), subject to an agreement to be concluded in accordance 
with Article 218 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union (TFEU). 
Article 8(1) of Regulation (EU) 2021/696 provides that third countries are able to have 
access to GOVSATCOM services provided that they conclude an agreement in accordance 
with Article 218 TFEU and comply with Article 43 of that Regulation.

(5) The participation of Iceland in the GOVSATCOM component of the Union Space 
Programme and in the Union Secure Connectivity Programme, and access to the respective 
governmental services of the Union and the Member States and of Iceland, will contribute 
to strengthening Europe’s role as a global actor and promoting international cooperation in 
line with the Space Strategy for Europe. An agreement with Iceland will strengthen the 
Union’s strategic posture in line with the European Union Space Strategy for Security and 
Defence. 
(6) The Agreement between the Republic of Iceland and the European Union on security 
procedures for the exchange of classified information4 entered into force on 1 March 2007. 
(7) Protocol 31 to the EEA Agreement on cooperation in specific fields outside the four 
freedoms will be amended by a decision of the EEA Joint Committee. That decision will 
include provisions on institutional and financial aspects as well as on data protection. 
(8) Negotiations should be opened with a view to concluding an agreement with Iceland laying 
down the rules for the participation of Iceland in the GOVSATCOM component of the 
Union Space Programme and in the Union Secure Connectivity Programme, and for access 
to the respective governmental services of the Union and the Member States and of 
Iceland,

Article 1 
1. The Commission is hereby authorised to open negotiations for an agreement with Iceland 
laying down the rules for the participation of Iceland in the GOVSATCOM component of 
the Union Space Programme and in the Union Secure Connectivity Programme, and for 
access to the respective governmental services of the Union and the Member States and of 
Iceland. 
2. The negotiations shall be conducted on the basis of the negotiating directives of the 
Council set out in the addendum to this Decision. 
Article 2 
The Commission is hereby nominated as the Union negotiator.

 COMPET.2  EN 
 
Article 3 
The negotiations shall be conducted in consultation with the Council Working Party on Space, 
which is hereby designated as the special committee provided for in Article 218(4) TFEU, and in 
accordance with the directives contained in the addendum to this Decision, subject to any directives 
which the Council may subsequently issue to the Commission. 
Article 4 
This Decision is addressed to the Commission.
