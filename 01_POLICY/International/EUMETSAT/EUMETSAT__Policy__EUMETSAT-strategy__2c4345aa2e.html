Strategy

 

EUMETSAT’s vision: a globally independent, trusted and leading operational agency for weather, climate and Earth system monitoring from space

The EUMETSAT Strategy 2040 provides the direction and scope of our activities over the long term, with the aim of maximising the benefits to those who use EUMETSAT's data, while accounting for any changes in their requirements.

Last Updated

12 October 2026

Published on

19 March 2020

EUMETSAT's vision is to be a globally independent, trusted and leading operational agency in weather, climate and Earth system monitoring from space, fulfilling the objectives of its Convention. Working closely with its Member States and the wider European Meteorological Infrastructure, it delivers observations that help protect lives, livelihoods and property, support economic activities and inform decision making across Europe.

EUMETSAT's satellites observe the Earth’s atmosphere, oceans, land and ice, providing the data behind everyday weather forecasts and the long-term records used to monitor the climate. Crucially, these data strengthen the early warning of severe weather, helping national meteorological services alert communities and respond to storms, floods, wildfires and other hazards. Drawing on the expertise of its headquarters in Darmstadt, Germany, and its network of Satellite Application Facilities, EUMETSAT helps its Member States build Europe's resilience to extreme weather and climate change.

Strategy 2040

EUMETSAT operates in a fast-changing world, influenced by environmental, economic and geopolitical shifts that are reshaping what its Member States need. Extreme weather is becoming more frequent and severe, and reliable observations and forecasting have never mattered more, both to protect people today and to inform the global response to climate change. At the same time, a more uncertain geopolitical landscape and the continued need to deliver strong value for public investment make independence, resilience, efficiency and cost-effectiveness more important than ever. EUMETSAT's Strategy 2040 positions the organisation to meet these challenges, building on decades of delivery to serve the national meteorological and hydrological services (NMHS) of its Member States and support global climate action through to 2040 and beyond.

The Strategy 2040 features eight strategic goals operationalised through three mutually reinforcing pillars. Pillar 1 delivers the core EUMETSAT’s mission of high-quality, reliable observations and services, while Pillars 2 and 3, supporting Pillar 1, strengthen the partnerships, capabilities and enabling conditions required to sustain continuity, enhance resilience and maximise value for Member States. The strategy is implemented in close cooperation with the members of the European Meteorological Infrastructure – the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts, EUMETNET and the NMHS of their Member States – and with the European Space Agency, the World Meteorological Organization, the European Union and international partners worldwide.

Pillar 1:

Deliver operational services and use innovation to prepare future systems

Strategic goal 1

Deliver new EUMETSAT programmes

Deploy new programmes on schedule and to specification, enabling the national meteorological and hydrological services of Member States to deliver improved forecast accuracy, enhanced severe weather warnings and expanded climate monitoring for Europe.

Strategic goal 2

Operate and deliver cost-effective, user-driven operational services

Maintain 24/7 cost-effective operational services that respond to evolving user requirements, sustaining the continuous data streams that underpin weather warnings, and climate and environmental monitoring so that Member States can deliver uninterrupted critical services.

Strategic goal 3

Plan for future resilient, flexible and affordable satellite systems

Plan future satellite systems that balance resilience, flexibility and affordability, securing long-term continuity of critical observations and climate data records into the 2040s.

Pillar 2:

Partnerships

Strategic goal 4

A trusted partner in Europe

Partner strategically with the European Union and support European policies to amplify the value of Member States' investments and strengthen Europe's collective position in addressing shared challenges.

Strategic goal 5

A trusted global partner

Establish balanced cooperation with international satellite operators, based on reciprocity and contributing to global Earth observation governance, securing access to worldwide data and maintaining European influence and relevance in the global observing system.

Strategic goal 6

Foster use of EUMETSAT services

Improve data quality, accessibility and user support to maximise the value of EUMETSAT's satellite investments, deepening use across European weather-sensitive sectors and scientific institutions while expanding reach beyond Member States to strengthen Europe's position as an essential international partner.

Pillar 3:

Organisation and People

Strategic goal 7

Towards a modern, effective and efficient EUMETSAT

Continuously improve organisational effectiveness and adaptability to sustain the operational excellence, technical expertise and institutional agility required to deliver reliable services, while managing costs, maintaining Member States' trust and evolving capabilities as required.

Strategic goal 8

EUMETSAT values its people

Maintain an attractive, inclusive workplace and implement a green policy, investing in the skills, engagement and continuous development of EUMETSAT's staff.
