SPECIALIST SECONDARY-SOURCE LIBRARY

Start with comparative policy and law, then procurement/industrial policy, financing, national studies, sustainability, security and downstream markets.
Research explains and critiques rules; it is not binding law. Industry-commissioned analysis is explicitly identified. Older studies remain useful historical context, not proof of current law.

Open 00-DOCUMENT-INDEX.html and search by title, issuer or identifier. JSONL and CSV catalogues provide source URLs, original hashes, roles, full titles and applicability notes. Original files are unchanged; TXT companions are derived search aids. Identical existing repository files are linked rather than copied.

The entity-coverage catalogue lists all 288 source/job entries from the procurement-derived scope; these are not 288 distinct authorities. Textual mentions and shared references do not establish authority-specific coverage. Missing specific sources remain explicitly unverified. See unresolved-sources.json for failed or rejected downloads. A missing template does not mean the authority has no template.

Naming: Entity__Document-Type__Official-Identifier-when-known__Subject__Content-Hash.extension. Full identifiers and titles remain in the catalogue when paths must be shortened.
