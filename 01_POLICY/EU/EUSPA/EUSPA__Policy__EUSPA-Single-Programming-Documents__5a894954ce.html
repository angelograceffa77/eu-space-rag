Single Programming Document (SPD)

The Single Programming Document (SPD) for the 2027–2029 period presents a structured overview of the Agency's planned objectives and activities to ensure transparency, accountability and efficient use of the financial and human resources allocated to EUSPA. This SPD serves as guidance to EUSPA’s priorities and activities based on the Agency’s operational objectives, providing stakeholders with clarity and assurance that the Agency is taking a systematic and consistent approach toward fulfilling its mandate by efficient use of allocated human and financial resources.
