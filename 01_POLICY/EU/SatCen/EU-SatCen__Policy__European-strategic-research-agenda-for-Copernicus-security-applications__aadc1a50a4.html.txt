Security applications are implemented in Copernicus Security Service (CSS). Page | Last updated: 19 Apr 2021 Earth Observation Copernicus provides six thematic services. Security applications are implemented in Copernicus Security Service (CSS), with the CSS split across three complementary thematic components: Border Surveillance, operated by FRONTEX, Maritime Surveillance, operated by EMSA, and Support to EU External Actions, operated by SATCEN. With the rapid progress on space technology and infrastructure, Research and Development (R&D) is key to close the gap between needs and the service portfolio offered by Copernicus. A structured approach to collect and analyse R&D needs is therefore needed to ensure that R&D is driven by operational requirements and optimise resources through increased synergies between various EU research programmes and initiatives and avoid unnecessary duplications. The Knowledge Centre for Earth Observation provides support to Copernicus in the new Space Programme (2021-2027). Upstream in the service supply chain, the KCEO will help to federate evolving needs for R&D. This will be done through close cooperation with the entities implementing the three CSS components, as well as maintaining a permanent dialogue with policy stakeholders across Commission Services and Member States. On the technology supply side, KCEO will work with experts across Europe, ESA, National Space Agencies and research organisations to map relevant R&D activities with the potential to fill the gaps between end-user requirements and service capabilities. Identification and monitoring of progress of relevant R&D activities – in particular those under Horizon Europe and National Programmes – and assessment of technology maturity will allow definition of roadmaps for transition from development to operations. The output of the work above will feed a Strategic Research Agenda (SRA) for CSS, which will be used as a structured input in the definition of Research Programmes at EU level (notably Horizon Europe).
