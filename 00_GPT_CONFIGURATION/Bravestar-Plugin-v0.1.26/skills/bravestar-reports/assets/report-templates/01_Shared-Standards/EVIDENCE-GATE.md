# Evidence gate

1. Record company identity, selected command, scope, languages and decision.
2. Resolve applicable GitHub registry, manifest, generation and all relevant parts/documents. Complete traversal where necessary to identify relevant records. Read relevant documents and annexes in full.
3. Log planned versus read files, versions, hashes where actually checked, extraction completeness and unread content. Search previews and samples are insufficient. Mark incomplete review explicitly and do not release as a complete final review.
4. Apply the specialist tool workflow in Bravestar instructions; respect GitHub-only restrictions. Record actual tool results, not intended use.
5. Reconcile official source identifiers, amendments, dates and controlling documents. Apply answer-time deadline rules, per lot/topic/stage and time zone. Do not infer closure from absence or count planned opportunities as open.
6. Link each material finding to public evidence and retain exact internal references in the separate archive. Preserve source data unchanged.
7. Keep mandatory requirements supported/not met/unverified. Essential missing documents or company facts make an eligibility assessment preliminary.
8. A complete repository reading does not establish complete external coverage. State limitations and uncertainty at finding level.

Use evidence-register.schema.json as the record structure. It records evidence and workflow, not hidden reasoning. No final completeness claim without the reading checklist and reviewed extraction limitations.
