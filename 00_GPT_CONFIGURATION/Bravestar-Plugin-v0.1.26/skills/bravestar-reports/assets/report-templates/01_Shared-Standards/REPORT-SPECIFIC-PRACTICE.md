# Report-specific business practice

Use the selected template and its exact entry in template-profiles.json. Read its cited benchmarks in TEMPLATE-BENCHMARKS.md. These report profiles are Bravestar writing requirements; they are not universal industry standards. When an official rule applies, record issuer, version, jurisdiction, applicable provision and why it applies. Check the current official source. Never transfer UK legal rules, US writing guidance or one ESA/EU call's limits to unrelated situations.

Follow REPORT-STANDARD.md for the five sections, presentation and official-form rules.

Before writing, confirm audience, decision, scope, reporting date, jurisdiction, relevant instrument and missing material inputs. Preserve the mandatory official-document intake for bid/no-bid and funding eligibility. Match the level of detail to Executive Standard or Detailed Report without dropping critical restrictions, risk or deadlines. If inputs are unavailable, disclose the exact limitation and keep conclusions conditional; do not invent values or turn a preliminary assessment into final clearance.

For each report, cover every applicable profile requirement. Merge overlapping tables rather than repeating them. In the private review, record the requirement ID, pass/fail/not-applicable, the report location, evidence reviewed and a reason. A not-applicable choice needs a specific scope reason. An entire report cannot be exempted from all checks. Missing material content blocks final release. This review is an explicit reviewer assessment, not an automated claim of substantive correctness.

For future new or revised templates, research an appropriate published example or authoritative guidance, inspect its actual content, record where it applies and any access limits, and create a unique profile with inputs, checks, matrix and release boundaries. Do not declare an industry standard without a verified standard and applicable edition. Preserve official forms and copyright. Update register hashes and benchmark dates; rerun structure and profile tests. Check whether the benchmark still applies when sources change or a new assignment's jurisdiction or scheme differs.

For final delivery, follow ../09_Quality-Checks/RELEASE-CHECKLIST.md. It explains the automatic checks, manual fallback and final-file review record.
