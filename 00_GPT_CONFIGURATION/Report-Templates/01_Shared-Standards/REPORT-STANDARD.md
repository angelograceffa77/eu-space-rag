# Shared report standard — version 1.5.2

Use the registered command template, this standard and the evidence gate together. Work on a copy; never put client content into this template library.

## Presentation
A4 page, 20 mm margins, navy headings (#16324F), teal accent (#087F8C), dark body text (#243746), white background. Body 11 pt, line spacing 1.35; title 26 pt, H1 18 pt, H2 14 pt. Use legible standard fonts (Arial/Calibri or equivalent), restrained colour, numbered pages and repeated table headings. Use the printable HTML shell as the baseline; inspect every rendered page before release. Long tables may use landscape pages or annexes. Never shrink text to hide overflow.

## Writing
Follow [Final editorial review before delivery](REPORT-FORMATS.md#final-editorial-review-before-delivery), the single writing and readability procedure. Apply it after substantive quality checks and revalidate the edited final version under the release checklist.

## Format and length
Follow [Report formats and length](REPORT-FORMATS.md). Offer Executive Standard and Detailed Report with the same evidence checks. Both explain context, evidence, reasons, conclusions and next actions.

## Sector adaptation
Maritime: operational area, vessel/port context, integration and maritime user's decision. Agriculture: crop/season, field scale, cadence, ground validation and agronomic decision. Insurance: peril, exposure, validation, claims/underwriting use and uncertainty. Transport: assets, latency, integration and operational action. Space hardware/operations: interfaces, maturity, qualification and mission constraints. Use only applicable dimensions and evidenced performance; do not invent sector standards or benefits.

## Report-specific conventions
Policy: adopted measures versus proposals, political incentives and alternative scenarios. Law: facts, jurisdiction, rules, reasoned application and unresolved legal questions. Procurement: lot-level requirements, mandatory gates and deadline zones. Funding: applicant/project eligibility, stages, cost categories and co-financing. Investment: thesis, evidence, risks and further diligence, without unsupported valuation. Clients: documented problem, integration and validation of benefit hypotheses. Contracts: numbered clauses and a separate drafting note; professional review before signature.

## General boundaries
Capability fit is not eligibility. A company lead is not a confirmed buyer. Directories may contain business units rather than unique legal entities. Never add overlapping category counts. Keep open, planned, awaiting-recheck, closed and awarded records distinct. Never use historical awards as current solicitations.

## Required report branding and sections
Use the approved medium-size astronaut-and-rocket image in bravestar-logo.png, with BRAVESTAR written on the rocket on the cover or first page of every generated report, including editable copies. Preserve its full name, motto and proportions; do not crop, redraw, stretch or substitute it. Embed the image so it remains visible offline. If the image cannot be included, hold final release and explain the limitation. For plain-text delivery, provide a branded PDF or DOCX rather than claiming text contains the image. On A4 reports, display the full image at 110 mm wide with automatic proportional height, centred on the cover or first page. Use the same size for both report formats and editable copies; reduce only to fit a narrower page. Keep the built-in white margins. Do not use the older ARIANE 6 lettering or earlier large/small variants. Visually check that the logo is legible and not clipped.
Use these main sections in this order for both formats:
1. Executive Summary
2. Analysis
3. Sources (where applicable)
4. Conclusion
5. Next Actions and Related Timelines
Put context, relevant evidence, justification and uncertainty within Analysis. Put relevant references in Sources: essential citations for Executive Standard and fuller references for Detailed Report. Omit Sources only when genuinely inapplicable, and record why in the private review; do not omit sources merely to shorten a researched report. State actual deadlines and time zones, or clearly labelled proposed target dates; never invent deadlines. For contracts, use these sections in the accompanying drafting report, not as clauses of the agreement. Standalone annexes belong to the branded report package and retain their appropriate internal structure. Keep the disclaimer and contact statement after the main sections.

## Identity, evidence and required statements
Show the client, Bravestar, report ID, version, date and evidence cutoff, scope, chosen format, draft/final status and confidentiality. Periodic reports also state their exact reporting period. Add a contents page when useful, normally for Detailed Report. Label charts and tables with units, currency and dates. Explain comparison methods and denominators. Apply the shared editorial procedure to definitions, notes and annexes.

Keep confidence ratings in chat and the private evidence record, never in client reports. Explain uncertainty and limitations in ordinary language. Risk severity may be shown when defined and supported; it is not an evidence-confidence rating. Do not claim human authorship or professional review that did not occur.

Include the full main disclaimer paragraph from DISCLAIMER.txt in every final report, editable copy, standalone annex and initial contract draft. Apply its separate contract warning only to contract drafts. Also include: For further details about this report or Bravestar, please contact angelograceffa77@gmail.com.

Initial contract drafts must also state: Initial draft—professional legal review required before use or signature.

For official applications or tender responses with a prescribed layout, prepare the required submission separately from the five-section Bravestar report. Do not change the official form to fit Bravestar headings. Keep contract clauses in the agreement and the five sections in its accompanying drafting report. Apply mandatory official formatting to the official submission; keep Bravestar branding and report statements in its advisory report and accompanying package without altering protected form fields.

Sources must support the actual findings, not merely name the template benchmarks. Next actions show priority, owner or proposed role, deadline or labelled target, dependencies and completion evidence. Keep sensitive internal paths and review records out of client appendices.
