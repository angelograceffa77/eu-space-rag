# Published benchmarks and applicability

Reviewed 2 November 2026. No single universal format was established for all 78 reports. The templates are original Bravestar editorial adaptations of relevant published guidance. Sources below are benchmarks, not endorsements, certification or blanket legal requirements. Verify the actual current call, contract, jurisdiction and recipient instructions before every assignment. No gated download or unread interactive content is represented as fully reviewed.

## POL

[European Commission Better Regulation toolbox](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-making-process/better-regulation/better-regulation-guidelines-and-toolbox/better-regulation-toolbox_en)

Type: Official methodology index. Scope: EU Commission; not a mandatory business-report format.

Lists impact, evaluation and consultation tools; use relevant tools, not every institutional procedure. Read status: retrieved-text.

## BUS

[HM Treasury business case guidance landing page](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/guidance-on-developing-business-cases)

Type: Official guidance index. Scope: UK public-sector business cases.

Links to the current June 2026 guide; not a Europe-wide report mandate. Read status: retrieved-text.

## HE

[Horizon Europe RIA/IA Standard Application Form v10](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/docs/2021-2027/horizon/temp-form/af/af_he-ria-ia_en.pdf)

Type: Official example application template. Scope: HE RIA/IA only; live call template takes priority.

Read the example form and work-plan tables; it explicitly warns that call forms may differ. Read status: retrieved-text.

## PITCH

[British Business Bank pitch-deck guidance](https://www.british-business-bank.co.uk/business-guidance/guidance-articles/business-essentials/what-makes-a-good-pitch-deck-for-investment)

Type: Business practice guidance. Scope: Startup investment communication, not a due-diligence standard.

Problem, market, model, evidence, team, funding ask and assumptions inform investor-facing reports. Read status: retrieved-text.

## LAW

[Georgetown Law Tips for Effective Organization](https://www.law.georgetown.edu/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/Tips-for-Effective-Organization.pdf)

Type: Academic legal-writing guidance. Scope: US legal-writing teaching; structure only, not European substantive law.

Use issue hierarchy and clear rule-to-fact reasoning; obtain governing law separately. Read status: retrieved-text.

## BID

[UK Assessment Summaries guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/procurement-act-2023-guidance-documents-procure-phase/guidance-assessment-summaries-html)

Type: Jurisdiction-specific official guidance. Scope: UK Procurement Act procedures where applicable.

Criteria and reasons help benchmark outcome reviews; do not impose UK processes across Europe. Read status: retrieved-text.

## ESA

[ESA legacy Business Applications documents](https://business.esa.int/documents)

Type: Legacy template index. Scope: Page limits proposal use to pre-25 November 2025 APQs.

Do not treat old templates as current; follow BASS and the actual tender action. Read status: retrieved-text.

## IP

[IPO Lambert Toolkit](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/university-and-business-collaboration-agreements-lambert-toolkit)

Type: Official model-agreement guidance. Scope: English-law collaboration; dated guidance.

Voluntary starting points with stated jurisdiction and update caveats; not a universal contract. Read status: retrieved-text.

## MARKET

[SBA market research and competitive analysis](https://legacy.sba.gov/business-guide/plan-your-business/market-research-competitive-analysis)

Type: Business practice guidance. Scope: US guidance; general analytical concepts only.

Demand, location, alternatives and barriers inform commercial research; use local evidence. Read status: retrieved-text.

## VALUE

[Strategyzer Value Proposition Canvas public guide](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-value-proposition-canvas)

Type: Publisher framework. Scope: Optional commercial framework; no certification claim.

Customer needs and offer fit require validation. No proprietary canvas is reproduced. Read status: retrieved-text.

## INC

[EBN EU BIC certification framework overview](https://ebn.eu/certification/)

Type: Industry quality-framework overview. Scope: Business incubators; not certification of Bravestar reports.

Support, ecosystem, startup outcomes and finance access inform portfolio checks; detailed audit standards were not obtained. Read status: retrieved-text.

## STK

[UK shoreline stakeholder engagement strategy appendix](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a79791eed915d07d35b5cc2/smpg-vol2-appa.pdf)

Type: Historical sector-specific example. Scope: Legacy coastal planning; method only.

Purpose, participants, methods and responsibility illustrate engagement planning; obsolete institutions are not reused. Read status: retrieved-text.

## POL2

[Better Regulation toolbox Chapter 2](https://commission.europa.eu/document/download/c0561a9c-d112-4d20-b1ec-145748e2c61c_en?filename=BR%20Toolbox%20-%20Dec%202025%20-%20Chapter%202.pdf)

Type: Official methodology. Scope: Commission impact assessments; adapted business analysis.

Problem, baseline, objectives, options, impacts and comparison provide a policy assessment benchmark. Read status: retrieved-text.

## POL7

[Better Regulation toolbox Chapter 7](https://commission.europa.eu/document/download/7b10fdd6-a992-4c65-b23d-185d45a5589f_en?filename=BR%20Toolbox%20-%20Dec%202025%20-%20Chapter%207.pdf)

## BASS

[ESA BASS documents](https://business.esa.int/bass-documents)

Type: Current programme template index. Scope: Relevant BASS scheme and stage only.

Lists current questionnaires and outline templates; official action versions govern. Read status: retrieved-text.

## CASE

[HM Treasury business case guide 2026](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a4390675b6406df58c14006/Guidance_on_Developing_Business_Cases.pdf)

Type: Official methodology and example tables. Scope: UK public investment; adapted to business planning.

Strategic rationale, options, affordability, commercial delivery and management inform integrated business cases. Read status: retrieved-text.

## APQ

[ESA BASS Activity Pitch Questionnaire](https://business.esa.int/sites/business/files/APQ%20ProjectName.pdf)

Type: Interactive PDF partially extracted. Scope: Specific call and its current form only.

Only opening instructions and field identifiers extracted. Dynamic content not fully readable; no full-form validation claimed. Read status: partial.

## SALES

[BDC business proposal guidance](https://www.bdc.ca/en/articles-tools/marketing-sales-export/sales/proposal-writing-tips-small-businesses)

Type: Business practice guide. Scope: Commercial proposals; no universal mandated format.

Client need, proposed solution, costs, timing, outcomes and exclusions inform sales reports. Read status: retrieved-text.

## NEWS

[Reuters Standards and Values](https://reutersagency.com/about/standards-values/)

Type: Editorial practice guidance. Scope: News verification principles; not an endorsement.

Attribution, verification and corrections inform monthly news reports; no proprietary text copied. Read status: retrieved-text.

## PSS

[ESA How to use PSS forms](https://www.esa.int/content/view/full/465593)

Type: Official costing guidance. Scope: ESA ITTs where required by current instructions.

Cost transparency and reconciliation inform bid reviews; current ITT decides forms and versions. Read status: retrieved-text.
