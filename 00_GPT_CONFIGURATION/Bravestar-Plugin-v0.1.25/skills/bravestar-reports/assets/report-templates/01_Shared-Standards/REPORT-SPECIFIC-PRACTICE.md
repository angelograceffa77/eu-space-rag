# Report-specific business practice

Use the selected template and its exact entry in template-profiles.json. Read its cited benchmarks in TEMPLATE-BENCHMARKS.md. These report profiles are Bravestar editorial requirements; they are not universal industry standards. When a formal rule applies, record issuer, version, jurisdiction, applicable provision and why it applies. Check the current official source. Never transfer UK legal rules, US writing guidance or one ESA/EU call's limits to unrelated situations.

Keep Executive Summary, Analysis, Sources where applicable, Conclusion, and Next Actions and Related Timelines as the minimum report structure. Put specialist content under these sections. Keep the logo, disclaimer, contact statement and the two format choices. For official applications, tender responses or contracts with prescribed layouts, prepare a separate compliant submission/contract alongside the five-section Bravestar advisory report. Do not alter an official form to force it into the report structure.

Before writing, confirm audience, decision, scope, reporting date, jurisdiction, relevant instrument and missing material inputs. Preserve the mandatory official-document intake for bid/no-bid and funding eligibility. Match the level of detail to Executive Standard or Detailed Report without dropping critical restrictions, risk or deadlines. If inputs are unavailable, disclose the exact limitation and keep conclusions conditional; do not invent values or turn a preliminary assessment into final clearance.

For each report, cover every applicable profile requirement. Merge overlapping tables rather than repeating them. In the private review, record the requirement ID, pass/fail/not-applicable, the report location, evidence reviewed and a reason. A not-applicable choice needs a specific scope reason. An entire report cannot be exempted from all checks. Missing material content blocks final release. This review is an explicit reviewer assessment, not an automated claim of substantive correctness.

Formal presentation: identify client, authoring organization, report ID, version, date/cutoff, scope, selected format and draft/final status. Use page numbers, consistent heading hierarchy, readable type, labelled tables/charts with units, currencies and dates, and define acronyms. Add a contents page when length or complexity warrants it, normally for Detailed Report. Separate assumptions, forecasts and estimates from observations; name the method and denominator for quantitative comparisons. Show risk severity only when defined and supported; it is not a confidence rating. Maintain the prohibition on confidence ratings in client reports.

Executive Summary states the decision and main constraints; Analysis explains material facts and evidence-based reasons; Sources links the actual relevant evidence, not merely the template benchmarks; Conclusion gives the recommendation and conditions; Next Actions gives priority, owner or proposed role, date or labelled target, dependency and completion evidence. Do not hide sensitive internal paths or review records in client appendices.

For future new or revised templates, research an appropriate published example or authoritative guidance, inspect its actual content, record applicability and access limits, and create a unique profile with inputs, checks, matrix and release boundaries. Do not declare an industry standard without a verified standard and applicable edition. Preserve official forms and copyright. Update register hashes and benchmark dates; rerun structure and profile tests. Review benchmark applicability when sources change or a new assignment's jurisdiction or scheme differs.

Before final delivery, run the existing final-artifact validator plus validate_template_review.py with the selected template ID and private review JSON. Both must pass. Review must bind to the actual final file SHA-256 and current profile version. Visual review and factual/legal review remain necessary. No automatic script can prove that a self-recorded review is truthful or complete.
