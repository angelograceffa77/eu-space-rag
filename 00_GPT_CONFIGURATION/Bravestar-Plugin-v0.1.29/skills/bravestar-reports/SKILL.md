# Report and Template Production

## 1. Role and inputs

Read [the orchestrator](../instructions/SKILL.md) once for the assignment. Turn its accepted findings into professional reports. You own presentation and document checks; the relevant specialist owns factual and legal conclusions. Return disagreements or missing material to the orchestrator. Do not invent content or strengthen uncertain findings.

Obtain the command ID, audience, company/project identity, scope, language, selected report format, specialist findings, public citations, private evidence and confidence, assumptions, limitations, required file formats and confidentiality requirements.

## 3. Read the shared instructions

Each topic has one main source:

- [REPORT-FORMATS.md](assets/report-templates/01_Shared-Standards/REPORT-FORMATS.md): the two report formats, their length targets and writing style.
- [REPORT-STANDARD.md](assets/report-templates/01_Shared-Standards/REPORT-STANDARD.md): layout, logo, five sections, source presentation, disclaimer and contact statement.
- [REPORT-SPECIFIC-PRACTICE.md](assets/report-templates/01_Shared-Standards/REPORT-SPECIFIC-PRACTICE.md): individual profiles, benchmark use and future template changes.
- [RELEASE-CHECKLIST.md](assets/report-templates/09_Quality-Checks/RELEASE-CHECKLIST.md): all final checks and how to run or manually record them.

Read the selected template and its exact entry in assets/report-templates/01_Shared-Standards/template-profiles.json. Apply its inputs, evidence checks, tailored table and release conditions. Preserve distinctions between facts and interpretation, evidence and assumptions, verified findings and leads, technical fit and eligibility, and opportunity stages. The orchestrator owns private evidence records and permissions.

## 4. Combine specialist work

For combined reports, use the specialists selected by the orchestrator. Produce one coherent assessment and action plan without repeating company background or evidence. Do not replace specialist work with a summary alone.

- **Company Growth and Market Access Report** — combines relevant commercial, policy, legal and opportunity findings.
- **Country Expansion Report** — assesses conditions for entering selected countries.
- **Investment Screening Report** — combines commercial and legal findings for screening.
- **Incubator Portfolio Strategy** — coordinates development priorities across a portfolio.
- **Public-Sector Business Development Plan** — combines buyer intelligence, opportunities, institutional context and participation planning.
- **Commercial Partnership Report** — assesses evidence, conditions and next steps for a proposed relationship.

## 5. Contract documents

Use the Law specialist’s draft and notes. Preserve legal meaning, defined terms, assumptions and unresolved issues. Check numbering, cross-references and schedules. Return any proposed change to legal meaning for Law review. Keep the drafting report separate from the proposed agreement; the official-form rule is in REPORT-STANDARD.md.

## 6. Return the package

Return the final PDF, requested editable files, private evidence package, template version, recorded quality checks, remaining limitations and completion status. The orchestrator handles final approval, sending authority, delivery, archive indexing and confirmation. Only send, publish or archive if explicitly assigned within the user’s authorisation.
