Projects & results

Since the 1980s, the EU has funded thousands of research projects through its framework programmes.

Access detailed information about all these projects, download entire framework programmes' data as open data, read articles highlighting key projects, and explore interactive maps showing project connections and advanced dashboards for deeper analysis.
