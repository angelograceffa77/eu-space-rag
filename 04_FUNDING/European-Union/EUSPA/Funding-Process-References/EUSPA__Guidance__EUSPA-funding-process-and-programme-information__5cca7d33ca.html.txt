Grants are direct financial contributions to finance either an action intended to help achieve an objective forming part of a European Union policy or the functioning of a body that pursues an aim of general European interest. According to the Contribution agreement signed with the European Commission EUSPA may award grants funded under Space Regulation and Horizon Europe Regulation . This page is exclusively dedicated to grants financed under Space regulation. For Horizon Europe funding please consult the Horizon Europe page . Calls for proposals This section of the website provides information about calls for proposals launched by EUSPA. The submission of proposals is only possible upon publication of a call for proposals that includes all the necessary information to introduce an application.
