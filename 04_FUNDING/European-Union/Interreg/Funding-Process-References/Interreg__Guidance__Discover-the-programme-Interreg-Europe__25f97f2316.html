    About our services

    Our Platform services provide a fast track to policy solutions. They range from simple exchanges to committed cooperation, and allow everyone to benefit without taking part in a project.

    Read more

    Our expert support services

    • Image
      Policy helpdesk
      Policymakers can get quick answers to any type of questions they might have on regional policymaking.
    • Image
      Matchmaking session
      Public authorities can explore good practices from across Europe in a two-hour discussion with selected peers and our Platform experts.
    • Image
      Peer review
      Public authorities can get tailored advice on their policy challenges through an in-depth two-day exchange with peers, stakeholders, and our Platform experts.

We create an environment and opportunities for sharing solutions and policy learning. We aim to make sure that government investment, innovation and implementation efforts all lead to integrated and sustainable solutions for people and places.

Programme actions

We help regional and local governments across Europe develop and deliver better policy in two ways.

Interregional cooperation projects

We bring partners from different regions across Europe together to exchange and transfer their experience on a shared regional development issue.
The Policy Learning Platform

Our team of experts use our project’s achievements to open up the benefits of the programme to all through policy-learning services.

Interreg Europe is the only EU programme that helps regional authorities across the whole of Europe cooperate on such a wide range of topics. Their projects are crucial to spreading good practices and making direct links between policymakers at the regional level.

Malgorzata Steckiewicz

Poland
