Download the logo Programme actions We help regional and local governments across Europe develop and deliver better policy in two ways. Interregional cooperation projects We bring partners from different regions across Europe together to exchange and transfer their experience on a shared regional development issue. The Policy Learning Platform Our team of experts use our project’s achievements to open up the benefits of the programme to all through policy-learning services. Interreg Europe is the only EU programme that helps regional authorities across the whole of Europe cooperate on such a wide range of topics. Their projects are crucial to spreading good practices and making direct links between policymakers at the regional level.
