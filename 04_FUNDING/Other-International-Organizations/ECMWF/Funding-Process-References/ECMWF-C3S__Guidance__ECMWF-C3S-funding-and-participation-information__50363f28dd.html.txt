When funded under specific European Union (EU) programmes, these grants also aim to further the interests of the EU. Grants are made available through a Call for Proposals — a public invitation directed at specific categories of applicants to submit proposals that align with the priorities outlined in the Call. Once awarded, grants are governed by the Grant Agreement signed between ECMWF and the Coordinator of the grant proposals. Current grants There are no current grants available.
