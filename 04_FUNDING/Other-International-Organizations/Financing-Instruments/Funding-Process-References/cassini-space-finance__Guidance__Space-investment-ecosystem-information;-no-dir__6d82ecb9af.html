About CASSINI

The CASSINI initiative (Competitive Space Start-ups for Innovation) is designed to boost space businesses in Europe. It supports entrepreneurs, start-ups, and small-to-medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in the space industry for the 2021-2027 period. CASSINI provides end-to-end support to help grow space businesses, from getting an idea off the ground to taking it to market.

A €1 billion EU seed and growth fund

Support and visibility with investor and partner networks

Coaching and mentoring

Cash prizes through innovation challenges and hackathons

Business acceleration

In-Orbit Demonstration / Validation (IOD / IOV) programme

Competition
CASSINI Hackathons and Mentoring

Online competitions for students, researchers and entrepreneurs to foster high-impact EU Space data ideas and concepts. Held twice a year focusing on sustainability and digitalisation goals. Winning teams receive cash prizes and access to mentoring.

Join Hackathon
Innovation
CASSINI Innovation Challenges

Boost Galileo and Copernicus data solutions with clear market uptake. Entrepreneur teams and early-stage start-ups compete to address real-world issues in sectors like health or mobility. Win cash prizes, network, and develop market-ready products.

View Challenges
Acceleration
CASSINI Business Accelerator

Coaching, training, and events for space start-ups who already have products on the market and pre-commercial success. Develop sales and fundraising skills, grow networks, and connect with partners and clients.

Apply Now
Networking
CASSINI Matchmaking Events

Dedicated events connecting start-ups, scale-ups, and SMEs to investors and bigger businesses. Receive pitch training, curated introductions, and 1-on-1 meetings to secure funding and form partnerships.

Register Event
Funding
CASSINI Investment Facility

Provides up to €1 billion risk capital to venture capital funds. Fosters investment in EU-based space companies, generating benefits for both investors and entrepreneurs.
