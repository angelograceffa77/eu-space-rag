Discover more About CASSINI The CASSINI initiative (Competitive Space Start-ups for Innovation) is designed to boost space businesses in Europe. It supports entrepreneurs, start-ups, and small-to-medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in the space industry for the 2021-2027 period. CASSINI provides end-to-end support to help grow space businesses, from getting an idea off the ground to taking it to market. A €1 billion EU seed and growth fund Support and visibility with investor and partner networks Coaching and mentoring Cash prizes through innovation challenges and hackathons Business acceleration In-Orbit Demonstration / Validation (IOD / IOV) programme Competition CASSINI Hackathons and Mentoring Online competitions for students, researchers and entrepreneurs to foster high-impact EU Space data ideas and concepts. Held twice a year focusing on sustainability and digitalisation goals. Winning teams receive cash prizes and access to mentoring.
