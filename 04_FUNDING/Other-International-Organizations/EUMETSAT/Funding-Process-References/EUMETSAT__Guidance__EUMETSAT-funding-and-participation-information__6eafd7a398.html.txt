supporting young scientists EUMETSAT, in close partnership with our Member States, supports young scientists in developing new uses and applications of our mission products. These EUMETSAT fellowships are close cooperative efforts with host institutions from across Europe. Last Updated 29 April 2026 Published on 09 April 2020 EUMETSAT research fellowships provide a unique platform for early-career scientists to advance their expertise in Earth observation. By engaging with cutting-edge data and technology, fellows contribute to innovative research that benefits society and enhances our understanding of environmental processes.
