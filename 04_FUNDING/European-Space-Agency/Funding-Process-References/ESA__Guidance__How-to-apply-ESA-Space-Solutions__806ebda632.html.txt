We’re looking for teams that have identified a market opportunity that’s attractive and has real potential for engaging customers. Motivation, business experience and industry domain expertise are key. But most importantly of all, we want to hear business ideas that are commercially viable, technically possible and involve either space tech or data. WHAT WE OFFER Zero-equity funding (from €60k to €3M+ per activity). Personalised support from a member of the ESA team. Technical & commercial guidance. Access to our network of partners. Credibility of the ESA brand. How to apply for BASS funding BASS is now part of ACCESS and to mark the transition we are offering a wider portfolio of funding opportunities , designed to support businesses depending on their level of maturity and commercial readiness. There are five different types of opportunities, falling under two main categories: Feasibility Studies: this de-risking stage explores the viability of new space-based applications and service concepts, consolidates the user landscape and requirements, and evaluates both economic sustainability and technical feasibility. Kick-start studies : Rapidly assess the viability and sustainability of new concepts Proof-of-concept studies : Design service concepts and develop a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) to consolidate market opportunities and user engagement Enabling studies: Foster early user engagement for space services facing barriers like regulatory challenges or public-driven business models Demonstration Projects: this implementation and business validation stages focuses on developing and validating space-based applications and services, testing their business models, and piloting them with anchor customers and users to support commercial launch. It also enables expansion into secondary markets. Pilot Projects : Integrate and pilot the service/product with customers in the primary market, demonstrating full integration in an operational context Growth Projects: Expand services into secondary markets, develop new service elements, conduct large-scale pilots, and support strategic partnerships for global market access We offer two types of funding calls across all frameworks: Open calls which are an always open invitation for businesses to reach out to us with their ideas Thematic calls which are time-limited invitations for ideas on specific topics, competitive invitation to tenders (ITTs), and contests View all live Calls for Proposals Application process Please follow the links below for more information on the specific application process for each type of funding opportunity: Proof-of-concept Studies and Pilot Project Open Calls Kick-start Open Calls For thematic calls, please follow specific esa-star links in each funding call.
