Do this in parallel with Step Two. Step Two: Contact your National Delegation Contact your National Delegation(s) to inform them that you are intending to apply for a Kick-start and to apply for a Written Authorisation of ESA Funding. Step Three: Submit your application Once you have written your proposal and included the Written Authorisation of ESA Funding in your cover letter, you are ready to submit your application via esa-star tendering .
