Energy Efficiency Measures in Public Buildings at the 
Municipality Level in Kosovo  
BMZ-NO. 200765941 
 
 
CLARIFICATION FOT PROSPECTIVE APPLICANTS (02.11.2023) 
 
-              In line with ITA 7 and 8. (7. Clarification of Prequalification Documents) and (8.  Amendment of 
Prequalifica¬tion Documents) 
 
-              and requests of potential applicants for additional clarifications and changes to the criteria; 
 
1.            Request for the Bill of Quantity (BoQ) for the  "Energy Efficiency Measures in Public Buildings at 
the Municipality Level in Kosovo"  tender; 
 
2.            Request to change the years of required experience for OHS officer from 10 years to 5 years of 
experience; 
 
PIU (Employer) in consultation with tender agent has reviewed the requests and notifies the applicants 
as follows; 
 
-              The Bill of Quantities will be provided to the shortlisted bidders together with the Tender 
Documents. For the Prequalification the Bill of Quantities are not required. 
 
-              The years of experience for the OHS officer is changed from 10 (ten) years to 5 (five) years of 
experience. (PIU has made this decision considering that based on the legislation in force, the first group 
of OHS Officer's was certified on 17.05.2017).
