Read about the Danish Euroguidance Centre Read about the Euroguidance Network Euroguidance: An international perspective Lifelong guidance is on the political agenda in many international forums. In the EU, the OECD and the World Bank, e.g., educational and vocational guidance is increasingly seen as a tool that may contribute to the promotion of social and economic development. The increased focus on lifelong guidance has resulted in a number of policy papers, surveys and analyses, handbooks and other publications.
