Application, admission and guidance

Get an overview of application and admission to higher education programmes plus study guidance.

Important dates in relation to application and admission 2026

1 February: The website Optagelse.dk opens for applications to higher education programmes

15 March - 12 noon (CET) (Optagelse.dk will close on 12 noon (CET)): Deadline for quota 2 applications. Deadline for all applicants with non-Danish exams.

5 July - 12 noon (CET) (Optagelse.dk will close on 12 noon (CET)): Deadline for quota 1 applications for applicants with a Danish Upper Secondary Education.

28 July: The applicants will receive a decision letter.
