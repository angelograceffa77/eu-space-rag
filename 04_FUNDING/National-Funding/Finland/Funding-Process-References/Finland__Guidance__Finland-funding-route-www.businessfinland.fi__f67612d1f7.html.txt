Front page Services Funding We accelerate the growth and internationalisation of Finnish companies. Business Finland finances companies that seek growth in international markets or develop new products, services and business models. Large companies and research organisations can also receive funding for joint projects with SMEs. Funding for public research We fund high-impact research and research collaboration between research organisations that create new innovations, expertise and sustainable growth. Research carried out together with companies creates solutions for the international market.
