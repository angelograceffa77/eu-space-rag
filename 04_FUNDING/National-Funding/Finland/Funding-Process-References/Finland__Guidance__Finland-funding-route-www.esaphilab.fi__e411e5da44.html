ESA Phi-lab Finland

Apply Anytime: Permanent Open Call

ESA Phi-Lab Finland is offering 4.4 M€ of space related funding and support of geospatial innovation projects between 2025 and 2030.

We conduct selections for research projects at least once a year.

Apply


Next cutoff day for the call is currently planned for second half of 2027.

THEMES OF OUR OPEN CALL

Sensor technologies related to space

  1. EO sensors upstream 
  1. Navigation sensors  
  1. Distributed sensor systems 
  1. Signals and systems innovation 

Advanced computation for geospatial services

  1. AI applications in upstream and downstream 
  1. Cost efficient computation 
  1. Algorithms and signal processing 

Novel digital geospatial services

  1. EO service innovation 
  1. Navigation and positioning services 
  1. Service platforms 

We are looking for

Groundbreaking science and technology development that have a potential in high-yield commercialisation

We are offering

Funding, research infrastructure, business and technical support for space related geospatial research projects. Upstream and downstream
with access to European wide ESA Phi-Lab network
