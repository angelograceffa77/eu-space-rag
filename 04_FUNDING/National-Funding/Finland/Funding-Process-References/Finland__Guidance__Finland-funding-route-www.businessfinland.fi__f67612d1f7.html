We accelerate the growth and internationalisation of Finnish companies. Business Finland finances companies that seek growth in international markets or develop new products, services and business models. Large companies and research organisations can also receive funding for joint projects with SMEs.

Funding for growth, internationalisation and innovation

  • On this page
  • Funding guidelines
  • Stages of applying for funding

Startup funding for growth

We help companies under the age of five to develop new innovative solutions, prepare for international growth, raise private funding, and bring solutions to the market.

See how we can finance your growth

R&D funding for renewal

Do you want to grow your business or boldly renew your ways of working? We fund companies' ambitious research and development projects that develop new products and services. 

See how we can fund your R&D work?

Funding for public research

We fund high-impact research and research collaboration between research organisations that create new innovations, expertise and sustainable growth. Research carried out together with companies creates solutions for the international market. 

See funding for public research

EU funding opportunities

Are you interested in EU funding, new partners and programmes? Our experts will help you find and utilise the funding offered by international programmes.
 

Find out more about EU funding opportunities

Stages of applying for funding

Business Finland's funding is intended for research and product development as well as for the needs of companies' business development and international growth.

Shortcut to services

How to apply for funding

1

Before you apply

Plan the project, define the goals and ensure the sufficiency of your own funding.

2

Apply for funding

Submit and submit the application in the online service. 

3

Approve the funding decision

Read the decision, read the terms and conditions and accept the decision in the online service.

4

Organize project follow-up

Arrange project accounting in accordance with the terms of funding.

5

Execute and report on the project

Report implementation and costs. Notify Business Finland of any changes.
