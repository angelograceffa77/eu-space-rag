Learn more Up in smoke? - Quite the opposite: a world-class Hungarian innovation turns CO2 into profit Up in smoke? - Quite the opposite: a world-class Hungarian innovation turns CO2 into profit While anyone can let money “go up in smoke”, making money out of smoke is a far greater challenge. The innovation of a Hungarian SME, eChemicles Zrt., does exactly this: it produces valuable raw materials from carbon dioxide. CO2 is entering the atmosphere in increasing quantities worldwide, acting as the primary driver of the greenhouse effect, and thus, global warming and the climate crisis. We talked about the invention – which received the 2025 DeepTech Award from the National Research Development and Innovation Office (NRDI Office) – with the company’s co-founder, Csaba Janáky. Learn more A HUF 20 billion boost for forward-looking energy innovations A HUF 20 billion boost for forward-looking energy innovations The call of the Jedlik Ányos Energy Programme supports the research and development projects of 35 winning energy R&D projects, and was implemented by the National Research, Development and Innovation Office. Approximately HUF 13 billion in funding awarded to half of the applicants will facilitate the implementation of innovative investments with a total cost exceeding HUF 20 billion. Out of the ten thematic areas announced, Hungarian ideas deemed worthy of support were identified in eight, with the highest number in building energy, energy efficiency, and solutions supporting system operation and reducing grid load. Learn more All calls and funding news Most effective projects All projects New paths in environmentally friendly water purification 13 May 2025 Reading time: 4 minute(s) The collaboration between IMSYS Kft. and HOLOFON Zrt. offers an environmentally conscious solution to the problem of water and wastewater treatment. The two Hungarian enterprises aim to explore the use of various ferrates in water purification and to develop new packaging material that enables the efficient transport of ferrate products. As a result of the project, implemented with the support of the National Research, Development and Innovation Fund, biologically contaminated water can be treated efficiently – from swimming pools to hospital wastewater. Learn more Artificial Intelligence helps medical diagnostics and drug discovery 30 January 2025 Reading time: 3 minute(s) The joint research and development project of GE HealthCare Kft. and the University of Debrecen will make medical diagnostics and pharmaceutical research more efficient with the help of Artificial Intelligence. Their solution, based on Natural Language Processing algorithms, supports the analysis and organisation of visual and textual findings, which can be used to target patients for inclusion in clinical research, among other things. Learn more A new milestone in personalised online medicine 13 November 2024 Reading time: 4 minute(s) The free-to-use telemedicine system developed by 77 Elektronika Kft. in Hungary is revolutionizing online remote medical services. The project, funded by the National Research, Development and Innovation Fund, aimed to personalise the treatment of patients with or at the early stages of diabetes. That’s why the Dcont® eNAPLÓ platform was created, where people with a Dcont® blood glucose meter can upload their measured data quickly and easily. These are supported by a number of graphs and statistics to help the patient’s orientation and the work of the medical specialist examining the data. Learn more Hungarian Participation in Horizon Europe Under the European Union's Horizon Europe research and innovation framework programme 2021-2027, a wide range of funding opportunities will be available to support research and innovation projects by international consortia to support individual research excellence, breakthrough innovations and international consortia to address major societal challenges.
