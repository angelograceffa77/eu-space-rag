The fund is under the auspices of the Ministry of Culture, Innovation and Higher Education. The Technology Development Fund offers four categories of corporate grants: Seed grants (Fræ/Thróunarfræ), and the Sprout , Growth and Market grants. Each grant is tailored to different stages of project development. Patent grants and Applied research grants are also available for universities and research institutions (usually in collaboration with companies).
