SUCCESS: Latvian students win three bronze medals at the International Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics
14.Oct

Latvian students won three bronze medals at the 19th International Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics in Hanoi, Vietnam, competing against peers from 65 countries, the news agency LETA was informed by the State Education Development Agency (VIAA).

A private European company reaches orbit with its own rocket for the first time
12.Oct

On 5 September, the Spectrum launch vehicle built by the German company Isar Aerospace lifted off from Andøya Spaceport in Norway, reached Earth orbit and deployed its satellites. It is the first time a private European company has delivered a payload to orbit using a launch vehicle of its own design, and also the first orbital launch from continental Europe. The mission was co-funded by the European Space Agency (ESA), of which Latvia has been an Associate Member since 2020.
