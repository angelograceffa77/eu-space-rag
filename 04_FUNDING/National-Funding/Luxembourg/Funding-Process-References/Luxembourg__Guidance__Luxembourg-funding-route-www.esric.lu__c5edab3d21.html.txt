Podcasts ESA & ESRIC Challenge Subscribe to our Newsletter Space Resources Week Contact Search ESRIC Start-up Support Programme #6 Open for applications The ESRIC Start-up Support Programme (SSP) is the first incubation programme in the world fully dedicated to space resources utilisation. Designed for DeepTech and SpaceTech innovators, it helps early-stage ventures validate their technology, build a solid business case and connect with customers along the emerging in-situ resource utilisation (ISRU) value chain from Earth to orbit and onwards to the Moon. Through SSP, ESRIC supports the development of solutions that will underpin a sustainable in-space economy while creating tangible terrestrial benefits in sectors such as mining, manufacturing, energy, agriculture and more. CALL FOR APPLICATIONS CLOSED Application deadline : 08 February 2026 - 11:59 PM CET Call for application Our suppport programme offers the opportunity to join the first incubation programme exclusively dedicated to space resources utilization projects. Selected projects will have access to non-dilutive funding in cash for an amount up to €200,000 . Find out more about the different phases and how much funding you can unlock. More info Who should apply? SSP targets start-ups and early-stage ventures that: Develop DeepTech or SpaceTech solutions relevant to space resources utilisation. Offer technology that can serve both terrestrial and space markets , or plan to expand from one domain to the other. Address challenges related to: Power and energy systems for space missions ISRU operations (prospecting, extraction, processing, storage, use) Infrastructure and mobility for the lunar and cis-lunar economy Enabling technologies for communications, sensing, and automation. Relevant application fields include, among others: mining, energy and power systems, 3D printing and advanced manufacturing, agriculture, marine operations, satellite communications, Earth observation, and infrastructure for lunar and cis-lunar environments. Application process The call is open to small and medium-sized enterprises, incorporated no longer than 5 years ago. They must be in the Space Resources Utilisation field, with a terrestrial and/or space application. During Phase 1 there is no need to be physically present in Luxembourg (remote) During Phase 2 the selected start-up needs to be registered and located in Luxembourg with R&D transferred in Luxembourg for the project in scope INTERESTED TO APPLY? Through the programme, we aim to identify early-stage space resources ventures with near term business models (space and non-space activities), as well as build a consolidated space resources ecosystem and we are always eager to hear from the community.
