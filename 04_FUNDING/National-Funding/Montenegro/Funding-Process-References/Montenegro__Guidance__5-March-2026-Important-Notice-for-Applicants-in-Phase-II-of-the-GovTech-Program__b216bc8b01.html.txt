Important Notice for Applicants in Phase II of the GovTech Program | The Innovation Fund Me Home Programs News About Fund FOI Contact Search Application Portal Home Programs News About Fund FOI Contact Me Application Portal News Important Notice for Applicants in Phase II of the GovTech Program Important Notice for Applicants in Phase II of the GovTech Program Published 5 March 2026 The Innovation Fund of Montenegro informs all interested applicants that, when preparing project proposals for Phase II of the Program for Financing Innovative Solutions in the Public Sector (GovTech), it is necessary to pay special attention to the submission of the mandatory documentation defined by the Program Manual . In order for the application submission process to be successfully completed, all requested documentation must be uploaded via the Application Portal , in the prescribed format and in the appropriate places within the application. The Application Portal technically requires uploading a certain number of documents so that the application process can be completed. If a certain document is not mandatory or is not applicable to your case, you need to upload a blank document in the designated place, so that the Portal allows the finalization of the application. For easier preparation of the application, we recommend that before the final submission you carefully review: Program Manual Application Checklist The documents are available on the Application Portal and on the official website of the Fund (LINK for documentation). Adherence to these instructions will contribute to a more efficient administrative check process and reduce potential uncertainties during the processing of applications. Table of mandatory documentation: The complete application documentation consists of: ELIGIBLE APPLICANTS a) Micro, small, medium enterprises (MSMEs), as well as newly established enterprises b) Research and knowledge-dissemination organizations or research organizations Application form – solution proposal + + Completed and signed applicant's statement + + Financial plan + + CVs of the project manager and key team members + + Presentation of the solution in a free format (submitted only by applicants who qualify for the second round of evaluation) + + Completed environmental and social impact assessment questionnaire + + Completed and signed applicant's statement on (non)recoverability of VAT + + Applicant's statement on related parties + – The applicant's balance sheet and income statement for 2025, or a cross-sectional balance sheet and income statement from the moment of establishment of the newly established enterprise until December 31, 2025 + – Statement on non-economic activities – + IDEAS PAY OFF! www.fondzainovacije.me For all current calls, trainings, and information, interested parties can stay informed via the Fund's website and follow posts on social networks: LinkedIn , Facebook and Instagram .
