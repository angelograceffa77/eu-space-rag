USB Environmental Intelligence and Emissions Grant Call

Total funding available: up to £2 million is available

Funding type: Research, innovation, and development

Who can apply: UK-registered end-user businesses, including SMEs, leading a consortium with at least one UK space supplier and any additional integrator or delivery partner organisations required to support project delivery.

Application deadline: 4:00 pm UK time, 11 December 2026

See the USB Environmental Intelligence and Emissions Grant Call guidance page to find out how to apply

ESERO-UK - Call for proposals

Total funding available: up to £4.5 million is available

Funding type: Learning and careers

Application deadline: 11:59 pm UK time, 20 January 2027

See the Call for proposals - ESERO-UK guidance page to find out how to apply

Call for proposals - Space Florida & UK Space Agency - Space Innovation Partnership

Total funding available: £150,000 from the UK Space Agency and $200,000 from Space Florida

Funding type: Research, innovation, and development

Who can apply: Businesses, universities and research organisations

Application deadline: 11 January 2027 (5:00 pm Florida time, 3:00 pm UK time)

See the Call for proposals - Space Florida & UK Space Agency - Space Innovation Partnership guidance page to find out how to apply

International Bilateral Fund – Call 3

Total funding available: up to £1.5m is available for each project

Funding type: Research, innovation, and development

Who can apply: UK businesses, universities and research organisations

EOI deadline: 14 September 2026 (optional but encouraged)

Application deadline: 4 November 2026

See the International Bilateral Fund – Call 3 guidance page to find out how to apply

Cyber security consultancy support, plus assessment and certification

Funding type: Development and consultation

Who can apply: UK-based SMEs involved in space sector activity (less than 250 employee headcount) with a manufacturing or operational presence in the UK (includes NI). Applications from micro/start-up businesses are particularly welcomed.

Closing date: Open until further notice.

See the Cyber security consultancy support guidance page to find out how to apply

ARTES: Invitations To Tender

Funding type: Research, innovation and development 

Who can apply: UK organisations and companies (UK Space Agency and ESA approval required) 

Funding: Dependent on activity

Deadline: Dependent on activity

This is an ESA-initiated programme offering up to 100% funding for developing early technologies and innovations. Invitations To Tender (ITT) are listed on the ESA website. 

Support for activities within the 2026 workplan can be found here.

If you have any questions, you can contact us directly at telecoms@ukspaceagency.gov.uk

Navigation Innovation Support Programme (NavISP)

Funding type: Research, innovation and development

Who can apply: UK organisations working in positioning, navigation and timing undertaking technology research and development focused on commercial innovation.

Funding:

Element 2026 2027 2028 Total
1 3.5-4 3.5 3.5 11
2 13 10 6.7 29.7
3 (more info to come for competitions) 4.4 4.3 4.3 13

Deadline: Open until further notice

Since 2017, the UK has invested over €100 million into NavISP to support the UK space PNT sector to innovate the next generation of space-enabled and space-supported navigation products, services and applications.

The UK investment in the programme is available to support UK organisations to undertake technology research and development focused on commercial innovation.

See the NavISP announcement of opportunity. Find out more about the NavISP programme.

Responsive Mode Call - Exploration Community Development

Funding type: Development and promotion of UK space exploration community

Who can apply: UK individuals and organisations

Funding: Support will be granted to a maximum of £50,000.

Deadline: Closed until further notice

The UK Space Agency will, subject to budget being available in any given financial year, make available funds to support activities such as:

  • community building activities

  • establishing networking opportunities

  • showcases and/or organisation attendance at appropriate conferences

Applicants should detail how the proposed activity benefits the UK space exploration community.

See the call and find out how to apply.

General Support Technology Programme (GSTP)

Funding type: Research, innovation, and development

Who can apply: UK organisations and companies (UK Space Agency and ESA approval required)

Deadline: Open until further notice.

The UK Space Agency is looking to provide support to companies and academic institutions across the UK in developing innovative technologies to grow UK capability. We are keen to engage with start-ups and SMEs (from both within and outside the space sector), as well as established companies and organisations. Collaborative proposals are also welcomed.

See the General Support Technology Programme guidance page for details on how to apply.
