The following have additional council-specific terms and conditions: Arts and Humanities Research Council (AHRC) Engineering and Physical Sciences Research Council (EPSRC) Medical Research Council (MRC). The terms and conditions you need to follow will depend on the funding opportunity. Both UKRI and councils reserve the right to terminate at any time. Your terms and conditions will be different if your project is funded by Innovate UK. Contact your monitoring officer if you have any questions. For a full list of policies, guidance and legislation to support good practice, see the good research resource hub .
