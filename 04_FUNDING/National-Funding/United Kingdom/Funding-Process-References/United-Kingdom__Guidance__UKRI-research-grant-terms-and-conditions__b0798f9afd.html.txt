From: UKRI Published: 5 April 2021 Last updated: 1 October 2026 - see all updates Documents UKRI terms and conditions of FEC grants HTML UKRI FEC grants: standard terms and conditions of grant guidance HTML Use of grant proposal and training grant information addendum (PDF) PDF , 121 KB If you cannot open or read this document, you can contact the Web team to request a different format (link will open in a new tab) . Details You need to comply with these terms and conditions if you are awarded a research grant. They include our expectations for how research is governed, including the safety and wellbeing of research staff, as well as the use of animals.
