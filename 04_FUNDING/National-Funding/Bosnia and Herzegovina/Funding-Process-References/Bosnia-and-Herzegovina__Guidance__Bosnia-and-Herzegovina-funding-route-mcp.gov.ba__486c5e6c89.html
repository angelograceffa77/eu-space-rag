FAQ in the science and culture sector

What grants are planned within the Ministry of Civil Affairs of Bosnia and Herzegovina?

The Ministry of Civil Affairs of Bosnia and Herzegovina plans the following grants every year: "Programs for the preparation of projects and potential candidates for funds from the HORIZON EUROPE fund", "Support for technical culture and innovation in BiH", "Grant for the implementation of bilateral cooperation projects in the field of science based on international agreements", "International cultural cooperation" and "Co-financing of cultural projects in Bosnia and Herzegovina".

How are funds from grants from the Ministry of Civil Affairs of Bosnia and Herzegovina allocated?

Funds from grants from the Ministry of Civil Affairs of Bosnia and Herzegovina are awarded exclusively through a public competition. This is the only way the Ministry can financially support projects.

When is the public competition for the awarding of grants of the Ministry of Civil Affairs of Bosnia and Herzegovina announced?

The public competition for the awarding of grants by the Ministry of Civil Affairs of Bosnia and Herzegovina is announced after the adoption of the Budget of the institutions of Bosnia and Herzegovina and the international obligations of Bosnia and Herzegovina. The tender is published through the media and on the Ministry's web portal (www.mcp.gov.ba), making it available to all interested entities.

.

Who can submit a proposal for awarding the Prize in the field of science in Bosnia and Herzegovina for the year 2023?

Proposals for awarding the Prize for Science can be submitted by academies of sciences and arts, universities, scientific research institutes, as well as individuals, through the ministries responsible for science and the Government of Brčko District of Bosnia and Herzegovina. One proposer can submit the proposal of only one candidate.

In which programs of the European Union in the field of culture and science does Bosnia and Herzegovina participate?

Bosnia and Herzegovina participates in several European Union programs that support the development of culture and science. These include Creative Europe, which supports the cultural and creative sectors, and HORIZON EUROPE, Europe's largest research and innovation programme. In addition, Bosnia and Herzegovina is active in the COST program (European Cooperation in Science and Technology), which promotes the coordination of national research work in Europe, as well as in the EUREKA program, which supports international cooperation in the development of innovative projects between companies and research institutions. These programs provide an opportunity to connect with international partners and advance projects on a global level.
