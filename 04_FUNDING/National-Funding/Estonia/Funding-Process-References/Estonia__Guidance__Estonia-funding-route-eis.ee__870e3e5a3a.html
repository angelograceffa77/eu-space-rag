Space Estonia

Reaching new heights with Estonia’s vision for smart, secure and sustainable space exploration.

We are developing the Estonian space policy and space business and promote international cooperation. Our goal is to provide Estonian companies with new business opportunities on markets contributing to high technology.

Estonia has been a full member of the European Space Agency (ESA) since 2015.

How can we help?

Collaboration opportunities

We mediate the invitations to tender of ESA

Consultations

Get in touch to consult with us if you're a project developer

Seminars

We organise information days, seminars, conferences, study trips, and match-making events
