1. What can I apply for? 
In the ARTES Spanish National Call 2024, the Agencia Espacial Española ( hereafter: 
AEE) has allocated  up to  22.4 M€ to support Spanish entities’ (companies and 
research institutions) Satellite Communications (Satcom) -related activities. 
 
The purpose of the call is to build and expand Satcom technology capabilities in Spain 
and strengthen the competitiveness of the Spanish  industry in the European and 
global satcom market. 
 
The call is targeting proposals for the  definition (only for the Strategic Programme 
Lines), development, verification, and demonstration  (only for the Strategic 
Programme Lines),  of products for the Satcom market, with the objective of future 
commercial exploitation of the products. An eligible product may be any hardware, 
software, system or sub-system, or may consist in a service. 
 
It is not a requirement that the activity cover all phases of a project. However, funding 
for additional phases through a CCN cannot be guaranteed at this time and will be 
subject to a new call.  
 
The proposed work shall not overlap with any previous or presently running ESA or 
National activities awarded to any entity in the Proposed Team. 
 
In 2024, the Call is divided into four categories (programme lines): 
 
• Category 1 : “ARTES C&G ” Generic Programme Line,  dedicated to telecom 
systems (e.g. end -to-end satcom systems, network protocols, propagation, 
resource management, air interfaces); telecom satellite platforms (e.g. attitude 
& orbit control, mechanical, thermal, power and propulsion techniques, devices, 
equipment and systems); telecom satellite payloads (e.g. antenna, repeater, and 
signal processing techniques, devices, equipment and systems); and g round 
segment of telecommunication systems (e.g. satellite user terminals, gateway, 
network, operations and mission contr ol techniques, devices, equipment and 
systems). 
 
A maximum budget of 5,8 M€ will be allocated by AEE to this category.  
 
• Category 2: “Optical and Quantum Communications Strategic (ScyLight)”  Programme 
Line, dedicated to demonstration and development of optical communication, photonics 
and quantum communication technologies enabling optical networks connecting multi-
orbit satellites with ground optical networks, as well as quantum satellite connectivity 
for applications such as QKD and entanglement distribution over long distance. 
 
A maximum budget of 5,5 M€ will be allocated by AEE to this category.  
 
• Category 3: “Space Systems for Safety and Security (4S) ” Strategic Programme Line , 
dedicated t o supporting European satellite operators , service providers  and the 
manufacturing industry, in ensuring new levels of security, robustness and resilience, 
which will become the standard for space communication solutions around the world. It 
4  
also aims to support the development of future satellite communications-based, end-to-
end safety and security applications for sectors such as maritime and aeronautical.  
 
     A maximum budget of 9,5 M€ will be allocated by AEE to this category, SAGA-related 
activities being explicitly excluded.  
 
• Category 4:  “Space for 5G/6G” Strategic Programme Line, dedicated to  supporting 
European satellite operators , mobile network operators , as well as industry  and 
academia, in the field of 5th and 6 th generation of communication networks (5G /6G), 
given the unique opportunities of orchestration and seamless integration of satellite with 
terrestrial networks (mobile, fixed, wireless) as an integral part of 5G and future 6G  
systems. 
 
     A maximum budget of 1,6 M€ will be allocated by AEE to this category.  
 
 
Read more about ESA’s ARTES programme here. 
 
If in one of the four categories the ESA b udget is not fully allocated, the remaining 
budget could be allocated in any of the other categories, at the discretion of AEE.

2. Who can apply? 
To apply for funding from the ARTES Spanish Space Technology Call 2024, applicants 
must meet the following eligibility requirements:  
 
• The applying entity (company or institution) must be registered in Spain , and the work 
must be carried out in Spain. 
• In case the proposal foresees the participation of subcontractors, the support of the 
Spanish Space Agency will be limited to the Spanish part of the work.  Any work to be 
carried out by non -Spanish sub -contractors must be self-funded by the latter or 
authorised and funded by their respective member states. 
 
In addition, the applying entity shall comply with the Special Conditions for the submission 
of the Outline Proposal (see section 5). 
 
Applicants should be registered in  esa-star for the last steps of the application 
process. It is highly encouraged to do this early. If the applicant is not already 
registered in esa-star, please use the following link:  
https://www.esa.int/About_Us/Business_with_ESA/How_to_do/esa -
star_registration_process

4. ARTES Development Phases target of this National Call 
The work proposed through this National Call can include one o r more of the ARTES 
Development Phases listed hereunder, with the indicated restrictions depending on 
the corresponding Programme Line.  
(1) The Definition Phase , in which industry is supported in the initial definition 
and top -level product design through technical studies. The objective of this 
phase is to produce a Requirements System Analysis. This Phase can only be used 
in any of the 3 Strategic Programme Lines under this Call.  
(2) The Technology Phase, in which the mitigation of technical risks is supported. 
The objective of this phase is to produce a breadboard, a prototype or an 
engineering model. This phase excludes qualification of space equipment and 
industrialization of ground equipment.  
(3) The 
Product Phase, in which the development, qualification, verification, and 
industrialization of the product is supported. The objective of this phase is to 
make the product ready for commercialization in its intended operational 
environment. 
(4) The Demonstration Phase , in which flight heritage and demonstration for 
space products, and the validation of ground segment products in the operational 
environment, is supported. The objective of this phase is to validate the product 
in its operational environment.  This Phase can only be used in any of the 3 
Strategic Programme Lines under this Call.  
 
Through these four phases, the complete product development cycle  is 
incorporated. A proposal can be aimed at any one or more of these phases . Starting 
the proposed work at the Definition Phase is not a requirement.

How do I apply? 
 
The application process for an ARTES activity is as follows: 
• Step 1: Ensure you’re registered in esa-star. 
• Step 2: Submit an Outline Proposal through the Open Space Innovation Platform 
(OSIP) campaign dedicated to the Spanish ARTES Call before the closing date. 
• Step 3: AEE, with the support of ESA, evaluates the received Outline Proposals and 
pre-selects the activities to be supported. The list of selected outline proposals 
will be published in the AEE´s web site.  
• Step 4: Successful applicants will receive a Letter of Support from the AEE for their 
activities and will be invited by ESA to submit a Full Proposal to ESA through esa-
star. 
 
Step 1: Ensure you’re registered in esa-star 
 
Please note that a pre -requisite for submitting any technology development proposal to 
ARTES, is to have completed the on-line dedicated questionnaire for being registered in esa-
8  
star as an entity wishing to do business with ESA.  
 
“esa-star registration” website: 
https://esastar-emr.sso.esa.int/ 
 
esa-star Registration User Manual:  
https://esastar-emr.sso.esa.int/Account/DownloadFile 
 
Please note that only the “Full Registration” allows a Contract to be placed with the selected 
Tenderer after a successful negotiation of a positively evaluated full proposal by ESA. It is 
therefore recommended to start this registration process promptly. 
 
Step 2: Submit an Outline Proposal to ARTES in OSIP 
 
The deadline for submitting an Outline Proposal to ESA is 10th February 2025. 
 
On ESA's ARTES website, you can download the outline proposal template  and the 
financial forecast workbook for a co-funded activity. Please note that the instructions 
in this document supersede the instructions in the channels in OSIP.  
 
Applicants must apply through the selected application process, where a 
company/institution submits its activity proposal. The application is called Outline 
Proposal. 
To submit your Outline Proposal, click “Submit Your Idea” at the top of the  ARTES 
Campaign webpage. 
 
Special Conditions for the submission of the Outline Proposal:  
 
• For all Call Categories, t he additional requested documents  indicated in the 
Annex 1 - Additional requested documents of this guidelines shall be submitted 
in the corresponding channel together with the Outline Proposal, preferably in 
one single separate pdf.  
• For all Call Categories an additional sub-chapter addressing the Impact for the 
Spanish Space Sector shall be included in the outline proposal following the 
guidelines in the Annex 2. 
• For all Call Categories, a cost breakdown shall be provided in the outline. Any 
expected expenditures (regardless of the amount) outside Spain shall also be 
listed, together with any expenditures in external major services (those above 
10K€). 
  
Step 3: Outline Proposals Evaluation  
 
After the deadline for submitting Outline Proposals, the evaluation and selection is 
conducted by AEE with the support of ESA.  The final decision is made based on the 
process described in this document . Evaluation criteria can be found in the Annex 3.  
 
AEE will publish the list of selected proposals in the AEE web site. For the selected 
proposals, recommendations for improvement may  be provided and shall be 
considered in the preparation of the full proposal.  
 
9  
AEE will submit Letters of support to ESA for the selected applicants.  
 
After the provision of Letters of Support, the AEE  is no longer involved in the 
application process and contract negotiation.  
 
Step 4: Submit Full Proposal to ESA 
 
Applicant entities that submitted  ARTES Outline Proposals that  have been 
positively evaluated will receive an invit ation from ESA to submit a Full Proposal  
following the ESA Star process. 
 
The Full proposal builds on the outline proposal taking into consideration the 
previous recommendations.  
 
In the event of a successful evaluation of the Full Proposal, ESA and the applicant 
engage in negotiations to finalize a contract.

7. Annex 1: Additional requested documents 
Documents requested in this Annex 1  can be provided in Spanish.  
Applicant entities  are requested to provide  the following, in an Annex included in 
the Outline Proposal:  
• The signed subcontracts or cooperation agreements or letters confirming the 
terms of the cooperation with the consortium members or subcontractors.  
• Any existing documentation showing reliably an interest of future customers 
or collaborations either for acquiring / exploiting the developed technologies 
or for exploiting scientifically the outputs of the project.  
In addition, applicants are requested to submit a 
Certified Statement  for the main 
proposer and each consortium member who would directly receive funding if this 
activity, justifying that: 
• The entities have not filed for bankruptcy and have not been declared insolvent 
• Are up to date with payments and comply with obligations regarding Taxes and 
Social Security, as imposed by current Spanish Regulations  
• Are not in situation of delinquency with respect the Spanish Administration 
and ESA 
• Are not already receiving public funding for the development tasks object of 
the present proposal. 
The information requested as a certified statement shall be uploaded on the 
corresponding OSIP channel under the heading “Certified statements ”.

8. Annex 3: Detailed Assessment Criteria 
 
1. Credibility of Business Plan (to be assessed by ESA): 
o Credibility of the business opportunity in the market context, including 
sustainability in the commercial market without further institutional 
support.  
o Improvement of global competitiveness of Europe in Satcom commercial 
markets; 
2. Quality and completeness of the product definition and technical 
implementation (to be assessed by ESA): 
o Product definition and development plan; 
o Evaluation of technical risks and credibility of the risk mitigation actions; 
o Verification and validation approach. 
3. Adequacy of the implementation approach (to be assessed by ESA): 
o List of hardware and software deliverables; 
o Schedule; 
o Adequacy of available funding for the activity objectives and phases; 
o Adequacy of the overall product development roadmap and expected 
funding 
4. Adequacy of the bidding consortium (to be assessed by ESA): 
o Completeness of the proposed consortium;  
o Company/ies technical and commercial relevant experience in all areas 
of the proposed activities; 
o Adequacy of the company resources with respect to the requested 
funding 
5. Spanish National Call specific criteria (to be assessed by AEE): 
o Return on investment for Spain: financial return to the Spanish space 
sector should be strongly demonstrated, including for the lead applicant, 
their partners and supply chains, and for the overall Spanish Space 
Ecosystem; 
o Potential of the proposal to grant Spain a leadership role in missions or 
in the development of payloads.  
o Leverage of the Spanish industry competitiveness, and the increase of 
market options of Spain in the international arena; 
o Feasibility of the proposal and programmatic risk; 
12  
o Consistency of the proposal with the strategic plan or goals of the 
company; 
o Mission context and relevance for the Spanish Space policy objectives: 
programme context, international partners, etc.; 
o Continuity from previously funded activities (if applicable). 
 
Projects that receive an assessment lower than 40/100 in any of the criteria above, 
will NOT be considered by the national delegation for a letter of support.
