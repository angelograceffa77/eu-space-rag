1. What can I apply for? 
In the GSTP Spanish Space Technology  Call 2024, the Agencia Espacial  Española 
(hereafter: AEE) has allocated  up to 15 M€ to support Spanish entities’ (companies 
and research institutions ) space related activities. 
 
The purpose of the call is to build and expand space technology  capabilities in Spain 
and strengthen the competitiveness of the Spanish  space industry  in the European 
and global space market. 
 
The call is targeting generic  space technology developments addressing any of the 
following application domains: E arth observation, science, exploration, human 
spaceflight, space safety, space transportation, navigation or generic technologies. In 
addition, the call has a dedicated category (see Category 2) for technologies targeting 
Lunar Exploration. 
 
It is not a requirement that the activity covers all phases of a project. However, 
additional funding cannot be guaranteed at this time and will be subject to a new 
call.  
 
The proposed work shall not overlap with any previous or presently running ESA or 
National activities awarded to any entity in the Proposed Team. 
 
In 2024, the Call is divided into three categories: 
 
• Category 1: GSTP Element 1 De-Risk Technology Framework is dedicated towards 
industry driven activities that are preparing and de -risking innovative technology 
developments and reducing the risk associated to them.  
 
In the GSTP Element 1 De -Risk Framework, activities between  100 K€  and  250  
K€ may be proposed.  
 
A maximum ESA budget of 1.5 M€ will be allocated to this category.  
 
Read more about the De-Risk Framework in section 4. 
 
• Category 2:  GSTP Element 1 Building Blocks  Framework - Technologies for Lunar 
Exploration is dedicated towards industry driven activities to develop enabling 
capabilities and the associated building blocks for space related systems and the 
associated sub-systems with the targeted applications for Lunar Exploration. 
 
In the GSTP Element 1 Building Blocks  for Lunar Exploration , activities between 
200  K€ and  1,000  K€ may be proposed.  
 
A maximum ESA budget of 5 M€ will be allocated to this category.  
 
Read more about the Building Blocks Framework for Lunar Exploration in section 4. 
 
 
 
4  
• Category 3: GSTP Element 2  is dedicated towards industry driven co -funded activities 
enabling the development of technologies and products for market competitiveness and 
sustainability.  
 
In the GSTP Element 2, activities between 200 K € (total budget) and 1,500 K€ 
(ESA  funding ) may be proposed . 
 
Activities proposed in this category are required to be co-funded by the Tenderer with 
a minimum of 50% of the total budget. 
 
A maximum ESA budget 8.5 M€ will be allocated to this category.  
 
Read more about Element 2 in section 4. 
 
Read more about ESA’s GSTP programme here. 
 
If in  one of the categories: GSTP De -risk Technology , Building Blocks for Lunar 
Exploration or Element 2, the ESA b udget is not fully allocated, the remaining budget 
could be allocated in any of the other categories.

2. Who can apply? 
To apply for funding from the GSTP Spanish Space Technology Call 2024, applicants 
must meet the following eligibility requirements:  
• The applicant entity (company or institution) must be registered in Spain.  
• In case of cooperation with entities in other GSTP Participating States, t he applicant 
portion of the activity must take place in Spain.  
 
In addition, the applicant entity shall comply with the Special Conditions for the submission 
of the Outline Proposal (see page 10). 
 
Applicants should be registered in  esa-star for the last steps of the application 
process. It is highly encouraged to do this early. If applicant is not already registered 
in esa-star, please use the following link:  
https://www.esa.int/About_Us/Business_with_ESA/How_to_do/esa -
star_registration_process

4. Call categories 
 
GSTP Element 1 – De-Risk Technology Framework 
 
GSTP Element 1 D e-Risk Framework is dedicated to preparing and de -risking 
innovative technology developments and to reduce the risk associated to them. During 
the activity potential added value of the development and the critical issues will be 
addressed. The results of th ese activities are intended to help orienting a follow -on 
development activity. 
 
In particular, the following tasks are expected to be considered in the activities under 
this category:  
• Identification and analysis of specifications for one or more space applications 
and the technical assessment of the development actions and associated 
schedule and cost. 
• Assessment of the potential benefits (performance, cost, lead time, risks…) and 
disadvantages of the potential solution with respect to the state-of-the-art. 
• Assessment of potential critical issues related to using a given technology for a 
specific application, using analysis/simulation and/or breadboarding and 
testing. 
 
The starting Technology Readiness Level (TRL)  of the proposed technology to be 
developed is expected to be 3 or above at  the start of the technology development 
activity and targeting TRL 4 or above  at the end of the activity . 
 
GSTP Element 1 Building Blocks Framework – Technologies for Lunar 
Exploration 
 
GSTP Element 1 Building Blocks Framework - Technologies for Lunar Exploration is 
dedicated towards industry driven activities to develop enabling capabilities and the 
associated building blocks for space related systems and the associated sub -systems 
with the targeted applications for Lunar Exploration. 
 
This category is focused specifically on the technologies for Lunar Exploration . This 
includes development of technologies, building blocks, components and test beds for 
projects for future Lunar missions. Including Platform, Payload, Ground Segment and 
Engineering tools. 
The results are intended to provide industry with a stronger foundation (i.e. building 
on this acquired know-how) in order to contribute to Lunar Exploration related system 
opportunities at national or international levels (institutional, commercial…). 
 
The developments proposed may  start from a proof -of-concept (via simulation or 
experiment) up to an engineering qualification model (EQM). 
 
The starting Technology Readiness Level (TRL)  of the proposed technology to be 
developed is expected to be 3 or above at the start of the technology development 
activity and targeting TRL 5 or above  at the end of the activity .

GSTP Element 2 
 
GSTP Element 2 is dedicated towards industry driven co-funded activities enabling the 
development of technologies and products for market competitiveness and 
sustainability. 
 
Industry is invited to submit proposals addressing one of the two technology and 
product development segments: 
• Segment 1: Market oriented opportunities 
• Segment 2: Strategic opportunities 
 
What are the Segments?  
 
• Segment 1: “Market Oriented Opportunities”:  Activities submitted in the frame of 
Segment 1  should address the development of new products or improvements of 
existing products for well-defined markets. The specific business opportunities should 
be clearly defined, along with the potential customers. In this segment, the Tenderers 
should provide a mature business plan. 
Companies submitting proposals in this segment should have a background in 
commercialising technologies in the space markets. 
 
• Segment 2: “Strategic Opportunities”: These activities should address cases that 
are commercially strategic from a market or an industrial (i.e. value chain/production 
process) perspective: 
o Strategic market goals include access to new applications/markets that have 
indirect commercial impact (i.e. they do not bring a direct commercial return 
but enhance the competitiveness, for example via a high-profile application/ 
market or by achieving market  diversity to reduce market dependence). For 
these goals, the business opportunity should be  well defined. They may also 
include entry into a new market, where the company needs to  develop, 
manufacture and test a breadboard or engineering model in orde r to attract 
interest and obtain detailed requirements from possible customers. 
o Strategic industrial (i.e. value chain/production process) goals pertain to 
companies that have  established products or technologies serving a given 
market. These companies may, for  instance, address obsolescence issues in 
the supply chain, apply new manuf acturing techniques  or develop an 
alternative (possibly internal) capability in order to replace an external source. 
 
NOTE: In the outline proposal template, please fill in only the part for the selected 
segment you apply. 
 
The starting Technology Readiness Level (TRL)  of the proposed technology to be 
developed is expected to be 3 or above at  the start of the technology development 
activity and targeting TRL 5 or above  at the end of the activity .

5. Conditions and the Process 
How much can I apply for? 
 
Category 1 – De-risk  
Technology development activities between 100 K€ and 250 K€ may be proposed in this 
category.  
The activities will be fully funded. 
The maximum duration of the activity cannot exceed 9 months.  
 
Category 2 – Building Blocks - Technologies for Lunar Exploration 
Technology development activities for Lunar exploration between 200 K€ and 1,000 K€ 
may be proposed in this category.  
The activities will be fully funded. 
The expected duration of this activities is no more than 24 months.  
 
Category 3 – Element 2 
For the Market oriented activities there is no budget difference between segments: 
activities between 200 K€ (total budget) and 1,500 K€ (ESA funding) may be proposed in 
both segments.   
Activities proposed in this category are required to be co-funded by the Tenderer with a 
minimum of 50% of the total budget. This requirement is mandatory and exception to 
the standard conditions of Element 21. This requirement applies to all entities participating 
in the consortium.   
Universities and research institutes are invited to support industry driven developments; 
however their participation shall b e limited to 30% of the overall effort.  Under this 
Element, universities and research institutes are not eligible to lead a consortium as Prime 
Contractors.   
The expected duration of this activities is no more than 24 months. 
 
How do I apply? 
 
The application process for a GSTP Element 1 De-Risk, Building Blocks and Element 2 
activities is as follows: 
• Step 1: Ensure you’re registered in esa-star. 
• Step 2: Submit an Outline Proposal to selected channel (OSIP Channel GSTP De-Risk, 
OSIP Channel GSTP Building Blocks, or OSIP Channel GSTP Element 2) in OSIP (Open 
Space Innovation Platform) before the ESA deadline: 28th October 2024. Extended 
ESA deadline: 18th November 2024. 
• Step 3: ESA and the AEE evaluates received Outline Proposals and pre-selects the 
activities to be supported. 
• Step 4: Successful applicants will receive a Letter of Support from the AEE for their 
activities and will be invited to submit a Full Proposal to ESA, through esa-star. 
 
1  The co-funding condition of 50% of the total budget supersedes the standard E2 
co-funding conditions. The co -funding table in the OSIP channel does not apply in

Step 3: Outline Proposals Evaluation  
 
After the deadline for submitting Outline Proposals, ESA and the AEE evaluates all 
proposals based on the De-Risk, Building Blocks,  or Element 2  evaluation criteria 
depending on the category applied. Evaluation criteria can be found in the Annex 3.  
In broad terms, the proposals will be evaluated on the following aspects: 
 
 
De-Risk outline proposal evaluation criteria : 
• Criteria 1. Technical and Implementation Proposal: Clear and credible definition of 
the technical objectives, key requirements, technical steps, and risks to be 
addressed in this activity.  
• Criteria 2. Innovation, Application and Impact assessment of the proposed 
development: Clear indication of the application and potential users of the 
technology including Impact on Spanish Space Sector. 
• Criteria 3. Management Considerations and Financial Overview: Clarity of the 
management approach and adequacy of the proposed costs with the work to be 
performed. 
• Criteria 4. Cost to Completion: Clear information about Cost to Completion 
including the Follow-on activities. 
 
 
Building Blocks  – Technologies for Lunar Exploration  outline proposal evaluation 
criteria: 
• Criteria 1. Technical and Implementation Proposal: Clear and credible definition of 
the technical objectives, key requirements, technical steps and risks to be addressed 
in this activity. 
• Criteria 2. Innovation, Application and Impact assessment of the proposed 
development: Clear indication of the application and potential users of the 
technology including Impact on Spanish Space Sector.

• Criteria 3. Management Considerations and Financial Overview: Clarity of the 
management approach and the adequacy of the proposed costs with the work to be 
performed in the Building Block activity. 
 
Element 2 outline proposal evaluation criteria:  
• Criteria 1. Market Context, Business/Strategic Opportunity and Impact 
assessment: Clarity and credibility of the market context and business opportunity 
(for segment 1) or strategic opportunity (for segment 2). Including Impact on 
Spanish Space Sector (for both Segment 1 and 2). 
• Criteria 2. Technical Proposal: Credibility and quality of the technical requirements, 
technical solutions versus activity objectives. 
• Criteria 3. Implementation Proposal: Credibility and quality of the proposed 
development plan, deliverables and schedule. 
• Criteria 4. Management Considerations: Credibility and quality of the bidder's 
background, experience and facilities. 
• Criteria 5.  Financial Overview: Credibility and quality of the cost breakdown by 
cost elements and cost by funding source. 
 
 
The evaluation and selection is conducted by  ESA and the AEE. The final decision is 
made based on the process described in this document.  
 
AEE will publish the list of selected proposals in the AEE web site. For selected 
proposals recommendations for improvement may  be provided and shall be 
considered in the preparation of the full proposal.  
 
AEE will submit Letters of support to ESA for the selected applicants.  
 
After the provision of Letters of Support, the AEE  is no longer involved in the 
application process and contract negotiation.  
 
Step 4: Submit Full Proposal to ESA 
 
Applicant entities that submitted GSTP De-Risk, Building Blocks, or Element 2 Outline 
Proposals which have been positively evaluated will receive an invitation from ESA 
to submit a Full Proposal following the ESA Star process:  De-Risk (Link here), Building 
Blocks (link here) or Element 2 (Link here). 
 
The Full proposal builds on the outline proposal  taking into consideration the 
previous recommendations. 
 
In the event of a successful evaluation of the Full Proposal, ESA and the applicant 
engage in negotiations to finalize a contract.

8. Annex 2: Spanish Space Technology Call specific criteria – 
Impact Assessment on the Spanish Space Sector 
 
Annex 2 indicates Spanish Space Technology Call specific criteria  that will be 
assessed in the Outline Proposal.  
 
Impact Assessment on the Spanish Space Sector criteria:  
• Return on Investment for Spain:  The financial return for the Spanish space 
sector must be clearly demonstrated.  
• Leadership Potential: The potential of the proposal to grant Spain a leadership 
role in missions or in the development of instruments will be assessed.  
• Competitiveness of the Spanish Industry:  demonstrate an increase in market 
options and position of Spain in the international arena.  
• Consistency with Strategic Objectives:  aligned with the company's strategic 
plan, as well as with the objectives of the Spanish space policy.  
Priority will be given to those proposals that demonstrate a greater impact for the 
Spanish space sector, which position Spain in a leadership role.

following chapters:
