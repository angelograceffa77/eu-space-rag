administrative authorisation to operate a taxi on a permanent or seasonal basis PROCEDURE STARTING AND MANAGING A BUSINESS Last updated: 10/04/2026 Summary Summary The Government is launching a call for applications for the granting of authorisations to operate taxis on a permanent or seasonal basis. Priority will be given based on a points system established by ministerial decree. Understanding the process Principle and conditions You may apply for an authorisation only within the framework of a call for applications for: Operation of a taxi on a permanent basis Operation of a seasonal taxi Permanent taxi licences Criteria for granting an administrative authorisation to operate a taxi on a permanent basis Eligibility requirements: Be of Monegasque nationality Hold the professional licence examination certificate Provide the required professional guarantees Have worked as a seasonal taxi driver, replacement taxi driver, chauffeur-driven vehicle driver, or private chauffeur for a minimum of six consecutive months on a full-time basis during the year preceding the application, unless a duly justified compelling reason applies. In such cases, the reference year will be the previous one Interested applicants must submit an application file for an administrative authorisation to operate a taxi on a permanent basis. Candidates are also encouraged to operate environmentally friendly vehicles, in line with the Principality of Monaco’s sustainable development policy, and meeting the technical requirements set out in Article 14 of Sovereign Ordinance No. 1.720 of 04 July 2008 on the regulation of taxis, chauffeur-driven vehicles, official city vehicles and motorcycles on request, amended . Incomplete or late applications will not be accepted. Only complete applications will be processed. Applicants who meet the required professional guarantees will also be subject to an oral assessment of their English and Italian language proficiency and will be invited to an interview to assess their motivation. Applicants who fail to attend the language tests without a duly justified compelling reason will be penalised. Failure to attend the interview without a duly justified compelling reason will result in disqualification. Seasonal taxi licences Criteria for granting an administrative authorisation to operate a seasonal taxi Eligibility requirements: Hold the professional licence examination certificate Provide the required professional guarantees Exclusion criteria: Having previously operated as a "Taxi de Monaco" licence holder (except for Monegasque nationals) Holding a taxi licence abroad under a lease-management arrangement Interested applicants must submit an application file for an administrative authorisation to operate a seasonal taxi. Candidates are also reminded of their obligation to operate a vehicle meeting the technical specifications set out in Article 38-9 of Sovereign Ordinance No. 1.720 of 04 July 2008 on the regulation of taxis, chauffeur-driven vehicles, official city vehicles and motorcycles on request, as amended . Incomplete or late applications will not be accepted. Only complete applications will be processed. Applicants whose files are deemed complete and admissible, and who meet the required professional guarantees, will also be subject to an oral assessment of their English and Italian language proficiency and will be invited to a motivation interview. Applicants who fail to attend the language tests without a duly justified compelling reason will be penalised. Failure to attend the interview without a duly justified compelling reason will result in disqualification. 1 Making your application Permanent taxi licences 1 Formulating the request Your application file must be submitted using the information form and the Sworn Declaration, both duly completed and signed. 98000 MONACO Or by email with acknowledgement of receipt to the following address: candidaturetaxi@gouv.mc 3 Mandatory procedures following grant of licence Once you have been granted a taxi licence, you must complete the following mandatory procedures: Procedures with the Business Development Agency Head over to at the Business Development Agency, between 9.00 am and 5.00 pm, to sign the certificate of non-transferability of your business permit. This formality is required for your business permit to be issued. If this is your first professional activity, you may be eligible for business start-up grant. Eligibility criteria and application procedures are available via the dedicated link: How to apply for a business start-up grant Procedures with the Driver and Vehicle Licensing Office Depending on your circumstances, please refer to the relevant sheet to find out the procedure and the documents you need to provide: First application for a professional licence: Information relating to this procedure is available via the dedicated link: How to request a professional licence to transport public passengers by car At your appointment, you must bring your business permit to obtain your professional licence Renewal of your professional licence : Information relating to this procedure is available via the dedicated link: How to renew a professional licence At your appointment, you must bring: Your business permit A payment of €32 (by cheque or bank card) In both cases, you must book an appointment online via the dedicated link: Booking an appointment with the Driver and Vehicle Licensing Office Additional formalities You must also carry out the following formalities: Register your vehicle Carry out the technical inspection of the vehicle (prior appointment booking is mandatory), except for new vehicles Send to the Driver and Vehicle Licensing Office, by email, a photograph of the vehicle after completion of the external signage, to enable your file to be updated and the vehicle registration certificate to be changed to commercial use Keep the certificate issued by the Driver and Vehicle Licensing Office, to be presented in the event of inspection on a public road Equipment and installations You must also carry out the following operations at the same time: Collect on-board equipment (including PDA) from Skyline company Book an appointment for the installation of mandatory equipment (meter and illuminated device) with an authorised company Collect the radio as well as recharge cards for electric charging points from the AETIM (Monaco Association of Independent Taxi Operators) office Present yourself at the external signage appointment, which will be communicated to you by the service provider designated by the Government Files PDF
