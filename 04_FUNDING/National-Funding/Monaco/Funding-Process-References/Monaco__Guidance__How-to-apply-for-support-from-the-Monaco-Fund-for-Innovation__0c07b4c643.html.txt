Starting and managing a business Support and funding Funding for innovation How to apply for support from the Monaco Fund for Innovation PROCEDURE STARTING AND MANAGING A BUSINESS Last updated: 05/12/2025 This fund offers support to Monegasque companies for innovative technological projects (involving real technological advances or the introduction of an innovative product to the market), through repayable loans or subsidies. Understanding the process Principle and conditions As part of its active pro-innovation policy, the Principality of Monaco aims to encourage high-potential, high-technology projects. The fund supports businesses based in Monaco that are developing innovative initiatives, whether through genuine technological advances or by bringing new products or services to market. Its aim is to help projects reach the market, create value and drive local economic growth. There are two types of support available through the Monaco Innovation Fund: Innovation Feasibility Support Innovation Development Support A preliminary meeting is required to confirm eligibility. Applications are assessed on the basis of: A business plan Three-year operating forecasts for the project 1. Innovation Feasibility Support This scheme aims to incentivise companies to commit to innovation by providing financial help with the costs of initial project R&D, including the following: Feasibility studies Acquiring the skills needed to validate the project’s component parts Expenditure financed: Payroll costs Market research Reviews of existing research and technology Literature and background research Design and development studies Instrumentation and equipment Industrial property Costs of finding partners And so on Conditions: The support provided may cover up to 50% of eligible expenditure With a maximum grant of €50.000.00​​​​ 2. Innovation Development Support This support is for businesses developing technologically innovative products, processes or services , and supports R&D phases that aim to deliver innovative solutions with clear potential for commercialisation. Eligible expenditure: Project design and scoping Technical and commercial feasibility In-house and external development costs Innovation consulting costs Costs of building prototypes Patent and trademark filing expenses Pre-production And so on Conditions: Interest-free loan of up to 50% of eligible expenditure Repayment may be deferred for several years 1 Obtaining and completing the form To apply for a payment from the Monegasque Fund for Innovation, you will need to download and complete the form: You can download it here: XLSX 203.5 kB Formulaire de demande - Fonds Monégasque à l'Innovation (n° 355-28-08-25) Collect it from the Business Development Agency 2 Submitting the application You can email your completed application to the following address: mbo@gouv.mc.
