    We deliver sustainable prosperity across Ireland

    Enterprise Ireland supported companies employ over 200,000 people around the country. Irish-owned businesses play a vital role in our economy, create sustainable jobs and drive prosperity in communities. From startups to SMEs to multinationals, we support Irish-owned companies on their journey as they scale, develop capabilities and expand their global footprint.
