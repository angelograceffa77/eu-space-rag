Slovenian Research and Innovation Agency

Funding research and driving innovation

ARIS is the national funding agency for scientific research and innovation in Slovenia. It creates the conditions for the development of knowledge, scientific excellence and innovation through public funding mechanisms, evaluations, analyses and other professional tasks. 

Current calls About the Agency

Key activities

Funding science

Stable funding, calls to fund research, staff and infrastructure.

Financing innovation

Incentives to develop innovation and transfer knowledge to industry and the wider society.

International cooperation

Researcher mobility, research partnerships and joint international calls.

System development

Standards, analysis, evaluation and cooperation for the development of the scientific research and innovation system.

From concept to application 

ARIS links funding for scientific research and innovation. It promotes scientific excellence, knowledge transfer and the development of innovations that have a long-term impact on society.

Creating the conditions for excellence in research

ARIS funds scientific research and ensures the conditions for scientific excellence and high quality research through public calls and peer review. 

How we support science and research: 

  • Clear calls and transparent criteria 
  • Peer review by national and international experts 
  • Support for researchers at various career stages 
  • Funding for projects, programmes, young researchers and infrastructure

Bringing knowledge to industry and society 

ARIS fosters collaboration and knowledge transfer between research organisations and industry, enabling new solutions to be developed and put into practice. 

How we support innovation: 

  • Calls and incentives for innovation activity 
  • Supporting cooperation between research organisations and industry 
  • Complementary funding for involvement in international programmes 
  • Advice and expert support for project applicants
