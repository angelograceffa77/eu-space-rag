The National Innovation Fund supports bold ideas that transform business. We provide grant funding for research projects, the development of new products, and the implementation of high technologies.

We connect science with business to build a more competitive Bulgaria. As a strategic partner, the National Innovation Fund provides comprehensive support—from the first steps of startups to large-scale projects by established leaders—successfully integrating them into the national innovation ecosystem.
