The revised rules will apply from Fast Track Call 5, launching on 31 August 2026. Key updates include: ✅ Dual-use technologies are now eligible. Companies applying through the Eureka Fast Track may submit dual-use projects, even if their original Eurostars-3 project was exclusively civilian. ✅ Updated evaluation framework , fully aligned with the 2026 EIC Accelerator, with greater emphasis on: Deep Tech and breakthrough innovation ; Intellectual Property (IP) ; Market Opportunity . ✅ Simplified evaluation process , with fewer assessment sub-criteria and revised scoring weights and thresholds. ✅ Additional clarifications regarding EIC funding pathways and updated eligibility thresholds for small mid-cap companies. The Eureka Fast Track enables successful Eurostars-3 companies to bypass the EIC Accelerator Short Proposal stage and proceed directly to the Full Proposal , while benefiting from expert feedback and EIC business coaching. 📅 The updated rules will apply from Fast Track Call 5 , opening on 31 August 2026 .
