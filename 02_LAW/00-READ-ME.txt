REFERENCE LIBRARY

Naming: Entity__Document-Type__Official-Identifier-when-known__Subject__Content-Hash.extension
Long subjects are shortened for Windows paths; full titles, old names and original identifiers remain in the catalogue. Short hashes distinguish versions, not official identifiers.

Open 00-DOCUMENT-INDEX.html. Search by country, entity, title, identifier or old name. PDF/DOCX/HTML originals are unchanged; text companions are derived search aids. Shared identical documents are linked to their existing repository location instead of copied.

Binding legal instruments, proposals, policies and guidance are different. Classification is a navigation aid, not a legal opinion or certification of currency. Source pages and linked candidates are not proof of complete coverage. Consult entity-coverage.csv and unresolved-sources.json.

Working scripts, generated datasets, manifests, checkpoints and functional filenames are intentionally retained to avoid breaking the existing architecture; all are listed in all-files-index.csv. funding data is excluded entirely. Original legacy indexes may contain former filenames; filename-register.csv preserves the mapping. No originals were deleted.
