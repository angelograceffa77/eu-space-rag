Ar ticle 1

1. This Decision establishes the European Uni on Space Threat Response Arc hit ecture (STRA) as a mec hanism to respond 
to space threats. This Decision sets out the responsibilities to be perf or med by the Council and the High Representative of 
the Uni on f or Foreign Aff airs and Secur ity P olicy (the ‘High Representative’) with rega rd to space threats.
2. For the pur pose of this Decision, ‘space threat’ means:
(a) a threat t o the secur ity of the Uni on or of one or more of its Member Stat es, ar ising from the deplo yment, operation or 
use of any space system; or
(b) a threat to the operation of any space syste m or to the pro vision of any space ser vice, the denial or inter r up tion of 
which wo uld har m the fundamental intere sts of the Union or of one or more of its Member States.
3. STRA ma y be used to respond to:
(a) a threat to the secur ity of an individual Member State or of a group of Member Stat es, including to the te r r itory , to space 
syste ms and ser vices or to other relevant capacities under the author ity or control of a Member Stat e or a group of 
Member Stat es; or
(b) a threat ar ising from the deplo yment, operation or use of the syste m set up and ser vices provided under the 
compo nents of the Union Space Programme or of the Union Secure Connectivity Programme, or a threat to the 
operation of any of those syste ms or t o the provision of those ser vices.
