The texts are reproduced in the form and in the language(s) in which they were received from the States and were not formally edited and/or translated by the United Nations. In 2022, UNOOSA launched a new platform, called Accessing Space Treaty Resources Online (ASTRO) , which serves as a database of national space laws, policies, and regulations of State members of COPUOS. This database is based on submissions made by States.
