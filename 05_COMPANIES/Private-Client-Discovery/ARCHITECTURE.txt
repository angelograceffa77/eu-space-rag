This is a separate research catalogue. It never changes Procurement-Master, Funding-Master, Investment-Master, supplier source records or existing daily policies. It is not a tender or funding opportunity catalogue. No external publication, outreach, automatic scheduling or deletion is enabled.

Sources: official SBTi downloadable company workbook gives broad global sector discovery; selected official business websites provide explicit private-ownership assertions and business activities, particularly in Africa. UN Global Compact search crawling is excluded because its robots policy disallows it. No login, private API, paywall or access-control bypass is used.

Coverage is partial and source-biased. SBTi participation indicates climate-target activity, not buying demand, incorporation status or private ownership. Website assertions are not independent legal due diligence. An exhaustive global private-company census cannot be established from these sources. Separate unverified leads allow future evidence enrichment without contaminating strict private-client results.
