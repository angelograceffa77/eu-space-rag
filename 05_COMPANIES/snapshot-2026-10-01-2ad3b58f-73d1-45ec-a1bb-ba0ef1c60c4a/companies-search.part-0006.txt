description: InnoviGuard AB doesn't ask you to trust your data and your commands. It proves them.

We build P55 Nexus, a proof engine for space communications. A satellite can go long stretches without contact with the ground. Most security models assume that
 connection is always there: a certificate authority reachable, a revocation list current, a key rotation schedule kept. P55 Nexus does not depend on any of tha
t. It lets a satellite prove that a command came from mission control, and lets mission control prove that data came from the satellite, using proof that stands
 on its own.

Each proof is bound to the specific hardware that produced it. Nothing captured from one system can be reused to impersonate another. Ground stations, satellite
s, and the links between them are proven independently, with no shared infrastructure to fail or be compromised.

InnoviGuard develops this technology and applies it across the space communications chain: ground segment, uplink command authentication, inter-satellite links.
 We are currently engaged in an ESA BIC Sweden project. InnoviGuard was founded by Carl Hendrix, who brings close to 30 years of experience in security.

P55 Nexus is designed to complement existing CCSDS standards rather than replace them. SDLS secures the link layer; P55 Nexus operates at the space packet level
, adding proof of origin and destination that SDLS's frame-level protection does not address. The two coexist in the same stack.
