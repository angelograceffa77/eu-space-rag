al Survey of Upland Habitats and the Irish Semi-natural Grasslands Survey. We also conduct Appropriate Assessments, Ecological Impact Assessments, habitat mappi
ng, management plan preparation, specialist botanical surveys and practical conservation work, such as rare plant translocation. Much of the work the company ha

All BEC Consultants staff are highly trained ecologists with a wealth of experience and, in many instances, a significant reputation in ecology, botany and cons
ervation management. The senior staff are full members of the Chartered Institute of Ecology and Environmental Management(CIEEM), which requires adherence to a
