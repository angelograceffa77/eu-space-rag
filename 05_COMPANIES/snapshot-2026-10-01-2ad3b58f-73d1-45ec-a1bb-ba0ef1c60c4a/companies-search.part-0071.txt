Founded in 1997, Tesera Systems Inc. (Tesera) is an employee&ndash;owned technology company. Tesera is 100% exclusively focused on delivering High Resolution In
ventory Solutions (HRIS).

In business for over 26 years, our interdisciplinary team reflects a unique combination of forestry and data IT skills and experience. We pride ourselves on pro
viding clients with responsive, exceptional services, high-quality, fully managed and transparent forest and natural resource inventory solutions.

Across Canada, the USA, Brazil and South Africa (in both natural and plantation forests), our team have now successfully completed 17 HRIS Enhanced Forest Inven
tories (EFI) projects on different forested landscapes covering almost 650,000 hectares, and Individual Tree Inventories (ITI) on a subset of 141,632 hectares.

OUR SOLUTIONS

HRIS integrates AI-based, machine learning and advanced analytics with Enhanced Forest Inventory (EFI) and Individual Tree Inventory (ITI) capabilities.

Tesera has designed HRIS to use fixed radius ground plots and a range of remote sensing data, including LiDAR, 4-band color infrared imagery, and multispectral 
satellite imagery. HRIS can accommodate terrain and soils, climate and other ancillary data.

HRIS uses advanced data science, AI-based machine learning (ML) and automation using Amazon Web Services to process forest inventory data. HRIS generates over 1
,300 features for use in developing models to predict accurate and reliable forest attributes including tree species, heights, diameters, volumes, forest stand 
structure, site productivity and many others.

Our unique and transparent approach to data aggregation and analytics empowers our clients to discover hidden and sustainable opportunities, and realize new val
ue from their forestry and natural resource assets. HRIS solutions are accurate, flexible and fully scalable&hellip; from small woodlands to extensive forest an
d natural resource landscapes covering millions of acres and hectares.

Over multiple projects and diverse forest conditions, HRIS is more accurate, with an increase in predictive accuracy of 66% (or three times more accurate). This
 means that in order to achieve the same accuracy with a conventional ground plot-based approach, one would need to establish at least 9 times the number of gro
und plots.

Our AI-based machine learning inventory solutions are extremely versatile and continue to support multiple data sources and applications. For example, HRIS is s
erving as the basis for determining forest carbon stocks, carbon offsets (sequestration and abatement) and will be used to model forest changes over time. HRIS 
is also providing an accurate and reliable approach to support several carbon and forest harvesting projects, ensuring independent data verification and adheren
ce to established standards and regulations. Additional insights on some of these projects can be found at: www.tesera.com/projects.

Our team is also advancing the automation of multivariate forest change detection using advanced algorithms and analytics to rapidly and accurately identify cha
nges in forest environments. This approach requires analyzing multiple variables and large datasets (time series satellite imagery, climate data and other envir
onmental indicators) to detect and monitor changes in forest cover, health and composition over time.
