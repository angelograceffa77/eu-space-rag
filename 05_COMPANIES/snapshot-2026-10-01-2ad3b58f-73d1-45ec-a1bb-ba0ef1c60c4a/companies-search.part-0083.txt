R-DAS) technology. Our core innovation transforms standard optical fibres into dense, large-scale arrays of virtual sensors capable of detecting and localising 
vibrations and strain with exceptional detail. Using HR-DAS, a single fibre optic cable becomes a continuous sensor capable of capturing vibration data at thous
ands of points along its length, with each point sampled at rates up to 1 million samples per second (1 MSPS). This level of resolution enables detection of hig
h-frequency acoustic signals, including ultrasonic vibrations, making it a powerful tool for non-destructive testing (NDT) and structural health monitoring (SHM
). Because of the fibre’s small size and passive nature, it can be seamlessly embedded into carbon composite structures during manufacturing, enabling real-time
, in-situ monitoring without adding significant weight or complexity. This makes the technology especially suitable for aerospace, space structures, and advance
d materials research, where performance, reliability, and early fault detection are critical. SensiPhi’s HR-DAS platform supports a wide range of applications, 
including: Structural integrity monitoring of spacecraft and aircraft, Ultrasonic NDT for defect detection in composite materials, Vibration analysis for launch
 systems and ground support structures, and Environmental sensing in harsh or remote conditions. By enabling high-resolution, distributed sensing with minimal i
nfrastructure, SensiPhi is redefining the way structures are monitored and understood, paving the way for safer, smarter, and more resilient aerospace systems.
