They are small and lightweight, combining the positive features of zero power passive dosimeters and the dynamic capability of active dosimeters.
Varadis RADFETs measure radiation exposure or dose together with an associated reader which converts the measured quantity in the sensor into an absorbed dose m
easurement for the user.
Optimized for radiation sensitivity to ionizing radiation: gamma rays, X rays and protons, Varadis RADFETs are ideal for space applications, including housekeep
ing of satellites, space radiation environment studies, shielding analysis and monitoring of radiation-hardened electronic components.
