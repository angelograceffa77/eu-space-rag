IGASPIN GmbH is a technology company specializing in GNSS resilience, signal intelligence, and software-defined receiver technologies. We develop solutions that
 improve the robustness, integrity, and availability of satellite-based navigation and timing in challenging RF environments.

Core activities
	•	Software-defined GNSS receivers (SDR) and software receiver development: design and implementation of flexible receiver architectures for rapid prototyping, 
advanced signal processing, and feature validation across multi-constellation and multi-frequency signals.
	•	Interference detection and characterization: monitoring and analysis of RF environments to identify disruptions, classify events, and quantify operational im
pact on receiver performance.
	•	Multi-constellation signal processing (“meta-signals”): combining information from multiple GNSS constellations to enhance accuracy, stability, and continuit
y of service under degraded conditions.
	•	AI-enabled analytics: machine-learning methods for anomaly detection, RF event classification, and decision support for operators and system integrators.
	•	R&D and prototyping: rapid development of algorithms and demonstrators, including integration into embedded and edge-processing platforms where low latency a
nd reliability are essential.
	•	Testing and validation: verification approaches and field testing concepts for performance evaluation in realistic scenarios, supporting technology maturatio
n from prototype to deployable solutions.

Relevance to ESA and space programs

IGASPIN supports space and downstream applications where dependable PNT is mission-critical—such as space operations support, aviation, critical infrastructure,
 and autonomous systems. Our SDR-based receiver approach enables fast iteration and transparent performance evaluation, accelerating technology maturation for n
avigation assurance and resilience across the European GNSS ecosystem.

Collaboration approach

We work with public institutions, research partners, and industrial primes to deliver measurable results: clear requirements, transparent KPIs, and engineering-
grade documentation. IGASPIN can contribute as a specialist partner for work packages covering software receiver/SDR development, algorithm design, system engin
eering support, prototyping, and validation.
