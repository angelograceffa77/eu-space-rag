structures. These ribs are easily combined with any type of base fabric, such as natural fibre- (NF), glass fibre- (GF) or carbon fibre
(CF) preforms. The main effect of the powerRibs is that they multiply the flexural stiffness of thin-walled structures without adding weight.

structure's lower weight, outperforming any given material in terms of sustainability.
