It was founded in 2012, with the goal of &ldquo;Launching Cyprus into the Space Era&rdquo; and building the next generation of scientists, that would see Cyprus

Today it has over 1000 registered researchers, scientists, educators and volunteers and over 250,000 followers on social media. CSEO has multiple spinoffs and s
ubsidiary companies, and supports and promotes startups and technological innovation.

CSEO's Space Cluster of companies is involved in over 10 funded space research programmes (Horizon, ESA, domestic and international).

- the International Astronautical Federation (IAF),
- the Global Experts Group on Sustainable Lunar Activities (GEGSLA),
- the Moon Village Association (MVA),
- the Mars Upper Atmosphere Network (MUAN),
- the Mars Society,
and many other international bodies.
